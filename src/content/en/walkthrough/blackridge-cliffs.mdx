The Blackridge Cliffs chapter strings five rewards down one cliffside: a Beacon, a boss, a crossbow, a Shell and a battleaxe. It also ends in a tavern. Efficient order below.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/walkthrough/blackridge-cliffs.webp" alt="The cliffside roads and village of Blackridge" loading="eager" /><figcaption>One cliff, five rewards, and a tavern at the bottom.</figcaption></div>

## The pass beacon

Teleport to **Widow's Overlook**, turn right behind the trial ground, and take the bridge up — the **Blackridge Pass Beacon** unlocks. Cleanse it, enter, and shoot the **gunpowder jars** with ranged attacks; the area is full of them. Open the chest for coins, cleanse the corruption, deliver the Ova — and grab the **Epiphany** behind the Beacon.

## The Flooded Village

Cross the bridge, straight ahead then right up the next bridge. Clear the area, **break the wooden wall bracing the stone door, and pass it from behind** into the building — the **Flooded Village** dungeon. Clear it, then take the marked door right of the drop: the **Grishash** boss waits below. His **Grishash Residue** drops on death.

Ride the device to the upper area, clear it, and follow the arrow. Loot the **Soggy Key** by the house, drop down, and unlock the red-marked building: the **Forgotten Crossbow** rests on its table. Ride the adjacent elevator out.

Back at the pass Beacon, take the left road up, clear the top, and cleanse the corruption ahead. Climb into the **Blackridge Cliffs village**, clearing as you go — the **Devout's Chalice** sits on the statue, with a meditation point beside it.

## The broken Beacon, the tomb and the axe

Turn left ahead and drop down; a **Mammon Coin** waits at the cliff. Back at the top, take the left road, shoot the explosive barrels, and collect three **Red Stones** — plus a **Heart Stone** from the villager's table. Leave through the village's left gate, clear the mob, and enter the **Shattered Beacon**: inside, the **Proxima Shell** unlocks with its memory scene.

Back on the surface, take the left cave into the **Collapsed Tunnel** — it holds the location hint for the **Triarch Repeater** (and begins collapsing once you check the map). Return to the broken Beacon, drop from its rear platform, and enter the **Royal Tomb** dungeon: break the crates, respect the traps and the suicide-adds behind the doors, and drop down.

Kill the elite, and the room hands over the **Veteran's Battleaxe** and a **Backplate Stone**. Ride the light back to the entrance and leave.

## The feeding ground and the road home

Return to the broken Beacon and take the marked area: clear the mobs, loot the **Thunder Shard** and a **Heart Stone**, then cleanse the corruption on the way forward. Clear the next area, cleanse the left passage's corruption, and descend — a **Red Stone** waits in the sarcophagus. Back up, the respawned enemies guard a chest holding the **Gloom Stone**.

Up the stairs and through the door, the **Feeding Ground** dungeon opens: step on the marked plank to fall through, follow the tunnel, and throw the switch. Up top, the right room's chest holds the **Scatter Stone**. Exit, take the left road, then straight at the fork — the left cave holds the **Shell-Nest Swarm** boss, with the **Justiciar's Stone** on the stele in the cave behind him.

Finish at the trial ahead — the **Unyielding Stone** is the prize — then follow the arrows over the bridges, left at the fork, and up the slope to the **One Legged Wolf tavern**: speak with the nun at Marrow Keep first if her dialogue line is pending, and the chapter closes.
