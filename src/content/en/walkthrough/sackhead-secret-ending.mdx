Mortal Shell II hides a second, stranger ending behind a burlap conversation. Follow it to the end and the game skips its own finale for something drier and funnier — plus an achievement for your trouble. **This dialogue commits you to the secret ending, so save before you begin.**

## When to start

The Sackhead stands just left of the exit of the Conjuror's Hideout dungeon in Gloomshade Grove. **Do this before you trigger the real ending** — the line cannot be completed after the credits, and the guide's standing advice holds: save first.

## The dialogue route

1. **Speak with the Sackhead** after leaving the dungeon.
2. **Answer "yes"** to his first question.
3. **When the conversation ends, pick up the Special Private Brew** behind you.
4. **Talk to him again — this time answer "no."**
5. **Return to the brew's pickup spot and put the Special Private Brew back.**
6. **Speak with him once more and answer "yes" to every remaining choice.**
7. **Talk to him one final time.** The scene plays, the secret ending unlocks, and the **Sackhead achievement** lands.

That is the whole ritual: yes, steal his drink, no, put it back, yes to everything. He appreciates the honesty. Probably.
