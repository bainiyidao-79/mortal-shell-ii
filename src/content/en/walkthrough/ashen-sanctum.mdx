The Ashen Sanctum is the citadel's inner sanctum: a short chapter built around blue-flame safety lamps, two Beacons, and the scholar prince at the end of it. It also secretly opens the endgame road.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/walkthrough/ashen-sanctum.webp" alt="The blue-lit halls of the Ashen Sanctum" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Extinguish the blue flames, or fight everything invincible.</figcaption></div>

## In through the gate

Enter the **Gate of Infinity** and run the sanctum's opening road, clearing mobs. At the marked area, clear the pack, then **double back**: up the stairs, **extinguish the blue flame**, and open the chest for the **Summoning Stone**.

Return through the gap ahead, clear the mob pack, and loot the **Backplate Stone**. Continue up the road to the top, then descend the long drop-chain to the platform where the **Bastille Annex Beacon** unlocks.

## The immobile sanctuary

Cleanse it and enter the **Mute Sanctuary**: light the blue flame here — this one is friendly, granting invulnerability — clear its wave, then its successor on the road beyond.

The left road hides a **portal**: cross it for the **Ram-Head Totem**. Ride the elevator to the platform atop the great creature, clear the mobs, and open the **Immovable Blade Stone** chest. Cleanse the Ova, rest — and a cutscene returns you to Marrow Keep, **unlocking the Unfinished Path endgame region**. (Teleport back; the sanctum is not finished with you.)

## Sonier and the scholar prince

Return to the **Bastille Annex** and take the stairs forward: fight, **extinguish the blue flame first**, then the elite **Gloom-Seeker Sonier** — his **Sonier Fragment** drops on death. Ride the elevator up, take the slope, and exit into the fork: left unlocks the **Bastille Courtyard Beacon**.

Cleanse it, face the **Servant of the Scholar Prince**, and deliver the Ova. Ride the portal forward — **Lazlo can be summoned** here — and enter the arena of **Sir Isaac, the Scholar Prince**. Kill him for the **Scholar's Wormstone**, and the **Scholar Prince's Chamber Beacon** unlocks. Cleanse it, deliver the Ova, and return to Marrow Keep: the citadel's interior is yours.
