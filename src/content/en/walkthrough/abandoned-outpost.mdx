The Abandoned Outpost is the Ruins of Mammon's longest chapter: bell puzzles, a royal tomb, three dungeons and two boss fights. It is also where [Lazlo, the Justicar](/shells/lazlo-the-justicar) and the [Obsidian Hammer](/weapons/obsidian-hammer) are won. Order of operations below.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/walkthrough/abandoned-outpost.webp" alt="The walls and roads of the Abandoned Outpost" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Bells raise roads; tombs hide Shells; mines hide hammers.</figcaption></div>

## The bell-ringer's house

From the **Abandoned Outpost Beacon**, take the right slope and clear the enemies, then ride the slope up onto the wall. **Strike the bell overhead** — when the road rises, enter the **Ringer's House** dungeon and ring its two bells the same way to raise the path. The chest holds the **Grudge Stone**. Outside, ride the slope down and cross with the device.

## Up the mountain, into the crypt

Climb the mountain road to the platform, take the stairs, and open the chest for **Nochte's Fang** — mind the trebuchet-armed enemies pelting the route. Cross the bridge, clear the foes, and pass under the arch (its left crates hold **Common Smuggled Wine**). The stairs up and the passage left lead to a lever that opens the great door — and **Ruk** waits on the statue with **Mammon Map Fragment 6**; the flanking statues hold **Martyrs' Ashes**.

The adjacent **High Lord's Court Beacon** unlocks. Return to the passage and ride the left device up, entering the room on the right: the **Royal Crypt of Mammon**. Straight through to the boss arena — the side alcoves hold loot — and face **Velen, High Lord of Mammon**.

## Velen and the crypt's rewards

Beat Velen and three walls of the chamber collapse. The flanking vaults' sarcophagi hide **four Mammon Coins** total, and the final chamber holds the **Lazlo Shell** and its memory scene. Leaving the crypt, the right road's room holds a **Revered Gland**, and a **Backplate Stone** rests on the stone chair by the exit.

## The Gate of Mammon and two dungeons

Out the gate, down the steps, ride the lever-elevator and cross with the device to unlock the **Gate of Mammon Beacon**. Its chest holds 2,000 coins; the elevator behind it descends into the district's lower warren. The glowing growth behind the house yields a **Red Stone**; the plank cart conceals a tunnel to the lower platform and the **Infested Miner** boss — the **Residue Bearer's Stone** is his price.

Continue through the passage, cross the right road's bridge, and take **Mammon Map Fragment 4** where Ruk waits. Climb the stairs to the top, exit left, break the wall boards, and clear the room — its balcony chest holds the **Splitting Stone**.

The other exit leads to the **Sentry's Grave** dungeon: descend, break the left boards, cross the room, drop from the passage, and face the **Enslaved Guardian** — clear his adds first for an easier fight. Victory yields the **Ballistazooka** sidearm.

## The Obsidian Mines finale

Out the left door, climb to the bandit camp (stock up), and loot the **Backplate Stone** from the left stone chair. Enter the cave, smash the right barrels for **Common Private Brew**, and break the left boards for the **Unassuming Key** and a **Red Stone**. Ride the elevator to the bottom, sprint past the mobs, and return to the Ruk area — the cliff road below the statue leads down.

Cross the bridge into the lower room (skip its dungeon for now), exit right, and ride the lever-elevator on the far side down to the **Obsidian Mines**: loot the Heart Stones from the side crates, drop through the central plank, and follow the rocks down into the boss arena. **The Prophet, Profaner of the Infinite** falls for the **Obsidian Hammer** and the **Paradox Scripture**. Ride the light out, break the boards up the wooden road, and climb back to the Gate of Mammon Beacon. Chapter complete.
