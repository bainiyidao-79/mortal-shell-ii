The Withered Shoals — the southwest Fall Gate of the Ruins of Mammon — is a district of portals and bird-skull landmarks, ending in a pit fight with a very large bug. This route covers the whole gate.

## In the gate

Enter the Fallen Gate and run the straight road, clearing mobs. Down the stairs, the inner road yields a **Mammon Coin** before the **Withered Shoals** proper opens. Sweep the snail mobs along the road; at the marked spot, go **right first** to find a portal — and a **Backplate Stone** beneath the giant bird skull.

## The portal loop

Take the portal: an elite **Strange Grishash** waits on the other side, dropping **Grishash Residue**, a **Backplate Stone**, and the **Mystery Remains**. Portal back and continue: clear the great clearing, sweep its **Red Stone**, and push on to unlock the **Bone-Stone Waterfall Beacon**.

Cleanse it and enter the **Shell-Shedding Hollow** behind it, clearing the mobs below and collecting the Ova. Before leaving the falls proper: behind the waterfall, a **sheep** and the **Sluggish Stone** wait in the back — grab both.

## The Nameless Path and the pit

Exit and climb the slope, left side: the mobs there guard three **Red Stones**. Then bear right along the road, clearing as you go, to the fork — take the crack into the crevice first, grabbing the **Heart Stone** before dropping down past four thoroughly unpleasant skull mobs.

Continue to the marked junction: left again, into a **Backplate Stone**. Push on to activate the **Nameless Path Beacon**, cleanse it, and enter the **Hateful Ravine** — the pipe mobs die fastest to ranged fire. At the pipe's end: right for the **Charged Stone** chest, left for the Ova. Deliver, return.

Unlock the Shell at the left-front of the Beacon as boss insurance. At the road's end, **do not jump yet** — sweep the deepest alcove first for the **Withered Gland**. Now jump: the **Hexapod** boss fight begins.

## The Hexapod

The Hexapod is honest but huge: simple movesets, telegraphed charges, and a backside that might as well have a sign on it. Dodge-first play beats parry-first here — the execution reward is not worth the risk. Stay behind it, punish the recoveries, and if the fight stalls, leave and return with the [Genessa Shell's](/shells/genessa-the-wayward) summon — the community's favorite crutch.

Victory pays the **Hexapod Core** Tarstone and unlocks the **Hexapod Burrow Beacon**. Cleanse it, collect the Ova, and return to Marrow Keep.
