The Valley of the Revered is the conquered temple's outdoor gauntlet: single-log bridges, archers with kickbacks, a sunken lake, and a conqueror at the end of it. Slow is smooth; smooth survives this chapter.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/walkthrough/valley-of-the-revered.webp" alt="The log bridges of the Valley of the Revered" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Log bridges, longbow archers, and a lake that repairs itself by bell.</figcaption></div>

## Into the valley

From the **Sisters' Gate Beacon**, pass through the gate behind it and run the long road into the valley — its sides line with **Martyrs' Ashes**, **Heart Stones** and **Backplate Stones**. Both forks rejoin the same road, so take either.

The valley's signature hazard: **single-log bridges patrolled by archers with devastating knockback.** A hit is a fall, and a fall is death. Clear what you can from range, cross slowly, and hold the guard button when a dodge will not make the window.

## Winter Lake

At the road's end, the stairs descend to **Winter Lake** — and the **Winter Lake Beacon** unlocks. Cleanse it, then enter the **Tomb of Chill Agony**: collect the **Immaculate Crown** in its corner, open the three doors in sequence, and beat the **Imprisoned Grishash** at the bottom for the **Fusillade Stone** and **Grishash Residue**. Cleanse the Ova and return.

## The bell bridges

Back at the lake, **strike the bells on the broken bridge** to repair it — the lake's signature trick, and the valley's shortcut system in miniature. Clear the lake mobs, find the **air-current device** in the corner, and ride it up to a portal.

Across: the fork pays a **Backplate Stone** to the left; the right **rings another bell, repairing a bridge and unlocking a permanent shortcut.** Straight ahead, three mobs guard a chest holding the **Pulse Stone**.

## Urig, the Prophet's chamber, and Zrog

The deep pit ahead drops onto the elite **Executioner Urig** — his kill pays the **Watcher's Stone**. Ride the air current up to the elevator but skip it for now: the stone chair on the right holds a **Backplate Stone**. Then ride up to unlock the **Prophet's Chamber Beacon**. Cleanse it and clear the **Ransacked Tomb** of its Tyrant Thralls, Ova included.

The road ahead ends at a portal and the **Conqueror Zrog** boss arena. He is famously manageable with a summon build — [Genessa's](/shells/genessa-the-wayward) duplicate keeps his attention while you work the flanks. Kill him for the **Conqueror's Reward**, and the **Conqueror's Tide Beacon** unlocks. Cleanse it, deliver the Ova, and ride home to Marrow Keep.
