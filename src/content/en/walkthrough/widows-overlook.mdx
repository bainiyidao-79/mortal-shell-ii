Chapter 3 returns to the Widow's Overlook with new eyes: a boss below the Beacon, a dungeon in the tower, a toad with a taste for mushrooms, and a graveyard where the chapter quietly ends. Everything is close — which is the point.

## The Beacon and the trial

Choose the crossing to **Widow's Overlook**. Enter its Beacon and claim the **Epiphany** — the currency for deepening Shell bonds with Zhileira. Below the camp, the crates hide a readable note; the stairs climb to the **trial challenge**, which pays the **Castigation Stone**. A **Mushroom** grows under the great tree right of the Beacon — grab it. It feeds the toad.

Exit into the cave and speak with the **Devouring Toad** inside. Outside, kill the cliff-top enemies for a **Heart Stone**, return, and feed the toad your mushroom for the **Marsh Pearl**.

## The Flesh Arbiter

Rest at the Beacon, then take the area below it: the **Flesh Arbiter** boss waits. Beat him for the **Arbiter's Prize** — the blood-curse Tarstone — and loot the **Revered Gland** from the stone figure to the right, plus **Gloomfire** from the nearby statue.

Descend the road and clear the lower mobs, then loot the **Scorched Doll** from the cliff-side statue on the right. Back up and left, clear the area and enter the building: the table inside holds a second **Scorched Doll**.

## The Martyr's Tomb

The building's lower level holds the **Martyr's Tomb** dungeon. Ride the elevator down, clear the cells, break the left crates, and press the door device. Read the room's note, open the chest for the **Obsidian Lathe** (a Tarforge upgrade for Franz) and the **Parasitic Stone** — then shoot the button above the door to leave.

Outside: left road, right turn, a **Red Stone**; the descending road past the mobs holds a **Heart Stone**. Ride the device platform up to Ruk for the conversation, and collect **Fainweald Map Fragment** — the overlook's map — beneath his statue.

## The camp, the graveyard, the chapter's end

Drop from Ruk's position to the forward camp: clear it, loot the **Common Private Brew** and the readable log, and enter the torch-marked stockade. Accept the prompt to **control the unknown Shell** — its memory scene delivers **Tiel, the Acolyte**, no fight required. The left side of the house holds another **Common Private Brew**; the right, a chest with the **Lethality Stone** and the **Ritual Grounds** dungeon — an elite-guarded climb worth a **500-coin** chest and an **Epiphany** at its trial.

Exit, meditate at the cliff overlook, and return to the Beacon: ride back to Marrow Keep, spend Epiphanies with Zhileira to deepen Shell bonds, hand Franz the **Obsidian Lathe** — the Tarforge learns **sidearm upgrades** — and let Chapter 3 close.
