The Prisoner's Domain is a vertical prison built to be died in: four Beacons, four bosses, and a chain of checkpoints that only reward players who cleanse on sight. This route runs the descent in the order the prison allows.

## Through the gate

Enter through the gate behind the **Silent Gate Beacon** and take the main road to the district's mouth. Note the reading material by the crates, climb the stairs, and **strike the marked wall** to pass — another note waits beyond the drop. Take the device crossing at the left passage's end and loot the **Backplate Stone** from the stele.

## The Sump Culvert

The **Sump Culvert Beacon** unlocks in the left cave. Cleanse it, ride the device platform, shoot the rope to drop the plank — a **Synaptic Vessel** waits past it. The upper road ends at another crossing and a chest holding the **Bulwark Stone**. Clear the area, cleanse the corruption, deliver the Ova, and exit into the pipe road beyond.

## The Gallows and the Legion

Shoot the explosive jars to topple the statue outside — climb it onto the wall, where the room holds a **Revered Gland** and the **Gallows Scaffold Beacon** unlocks beside the exit. Its corpse holds **Common Private Brew**; the corridor's switch opens the boss gate.

**The Spectral Legion** waits inside. Kill it, cleanse, deliver — then climb the Beacon's left stairs into the long ascent, cross the bridge, ride the elevator (shooting its rope on the way, for the loot), and drop from the passage's end to unlock the **Executioner's Pulpit Beacon**.

## The pulpit and the confession

Inside: open the left cell with your weapon, press its switch, clear the opened corridor, break the crates, pull the lever. The chest right of the second room holds the **Squall Stone**; the left room's switch opens the road back. Cleanse, deliver, cross the device — and the **Confessor of Tishna** boss waits.

Kill the Confessor for the **Confessor's Keepsake**. Restock at the pulpit Beacon — **a second boss follows** — then ride the arena's device up to Ruk for a conversation, grab the **Rupturing Stone** chest behind him, and drop to the pit's very bottom for **the Nameless Prisoner**.

## The bottom of the world

Kill the Nameless Prisoner for the **Captive's Scabstone**. The **End of Imprisonment Beacon** unlocks — cleanse it, carry the Ova home to Marrow Keep, and let the chapter close behind you.
