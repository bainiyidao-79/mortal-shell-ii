The Outskirts of Mammon introduce the game's second overworld region: a trial, two dungeons, a giant's empty cage, and the road that opens the Ruins of Mammon proper. Efficient order below.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/walkthrough/outskirts-of-mammon.webp" alt="The roads and camps of the Outskirts of Mammon" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Trial first, dungeons second, outpost road last.</figcaption></div>

## The trial and the Witch Pit

Fast travel to the **Outskirts of Mammon Beacon**. Take the left slope down and start the **trial challenge** — the glowing growth on the nearby cliff hides a **Red Stone**. Victory pays the **Enfeebling Stone**.

Behind the Beacon, descend to the camp below and open its chest for the **Forging Stone**. The cave at the mountain's right foot leads into the **Witch Pit** dungeon: drop from the platforms, clear the area, take the 1,000 coins and the **Red Stone** on the crates, and leave.

## Vlas and the cage

Ride the right-side camp road, cross the bridge, and turn ahead-left to trigger the **Vlas conversation**. Talk with him on the upper platform, inspect the **great empty cage** for a **Mammon Coin**, and shoot the rope ahead to drop the plank. At the plank road's end lies **Simple Ammunition**, with a readable note on the corpse beside it.

Double back, take the right road to the device crossing, and climb the slope through the right passage. Another **Ruk conversation** marks a **Map Fragment** beneath his statue; cross the bridge ahead of the statue and clear the camp — its crates hold **Common Smuggled Wine**.

## The Smuggler's Hideout and the road on

The camp's dungeon, the **Smuggler's Hideout**, hides a chest behind its waterfall — **Mammon Coin** again, plus two **Heart Stones** in the adjacent crates. Two enemies respawn on the exit path; expect them.

Return to the statue, cross the bridge behind it, and ride the road-end device across. Take the left slope down to unlock the **Abandoned Outpost Beacon**, open its chest for 1,000 coins, break the crates to drop into the lower room, clear it, and press the wall switch. Back upstairs, the opened room's chest holds the **Grudge Stone**. Cleanse the corruption, carry the Ova home — and the district is done.
