The One-Legged Wolf chapter is the game's tavern episode: a mine dungeon, a stage with a free instrument, a drunkard hiding the eighth Shell, and a toad whose eating disorder pays in daggers. Everyone in this chapter wants something. This guide gets it for you.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/walkthrough/one-legged-wolf-tavern.webp" alt="The tavern district of the One-Legged Wolf" loading="eager" /><figcaption>A lute, a drunkard's quest, and the game's best-hidden Shell.</figcaption></div>

## The mine first

Teleport to the **Mushroom Village Gate** and speak with **Grom** in the yard — the toad and cauldron beside him respond to interaction too. Behind the yard, cleanse the corruption and clear the gate's far side, then exit through the door into the **Derelict Mine** dungeon: break the door by the signboard, descend, clear, and read the corpse's note for the chest's location — 2,000 coins under the elevator. Ride the elevator out.

## The tavern and the lute

Exit the dungeon right into the rear cave; kill the elite, cleanse the right-side corruption, and light the **One Legged Wolf Beacon** on the left. Find Ruk ahead-left for the conversation and the map beneath his statue, then descend the mountain road into the **One-Legged Wolf tavern**.

Inside: talk to the NPC for **Common Private Brew**, loot the **Bard's Lute** from the stage, and meet **Gragu** — the drunkard with a request. His quest needs the **Heart of Vatra**, and the bar sells a **Stone Hand** to help you get it.

## The Heart of Vatra

Exit and cross the right bridge; at the road's end, ride the device across. Climb into the building, turn right, and loot the **Synaptic Vessel** beneath the statue — with a sheep (and a **Ram-Head Totem**) behind it. Up the stairs, left at the landing, and open the area chest: the **Berserker's Stone**. Return to the landing, climb higher, and take the **Heart of Vatra** from the forward statue.

**The statue wakes when you loot it.** Sprint back to the tavern the way you came and hand the Heart to Gragu: the **Gragu Shell** unlocks with its memory scene, and the **Heartless One** achievement lands.

## The toad loop

Teleport to **Widow's Overlook** and feed the toad a **Mushroom**. Rest at the Beacon to respawn the map, and repeat — a few rounds of feeding makes the toad cough up the **Venomous Dagger** weapon.

Teleport to the Mushroom Village Gate and hand the dagger to **Grom** — two enemies spawn behind you when you do. Then back to the toad: **Helga** now stands beside it; give her the **Common Private Brew**. Rest, return, and repeat three times. Finally, interact with the **small toad** beside her — the quest closes, and the chapter with it.
