The Ruins of Nochte blend a dying village with the game's strangest Shell ritual: three stone circles that must run red before a corpse gives up its body. This route covers all of it, plus the district's two dungeons and the gate boss at the end.

## The descent and the village

Fast travel to the Gloomshade Grove Beacon, drop straight down, and follow the left cliff — the road switchbacks through an arch and uphill. Loot the **Red Stone** from the sarcophagus on the platform, drop from its left, and clear the village. Behind the right-hand building, boards conceal the **Disciples' Hideout** dungeon: an elite guards a chest of five **Heart Stones** (plus coins behind it).

## The catfish and the Mango Sanctuary

Above the village, the waterfall cave hides loot — and the **catfish** who wants the **Mother's Flesh**. Feed it and be swallowed into the Mango Sanctuary: ride the descending road, grab the **Mango**, and touch the light to return. Past the arches, the **Ruk conversation** marks **Fainweald Map Fragment 1** beneath the statue.

## The Beacon, the illusion and Smert

Down the slope, the **Ruins of Nochte Beacon** unlocks. Cleanse it, enter, and **strike the wall with your weapon** — the illusion parts. Climb, ride the device up, clear the upper floor, and open the chest for the **Hag Stone**. Drop down, cleanse the corruption, return the Ova.

Then the ritual: past the Beacon's left arch, **lure enemies into the three stone circles until each runs red.** All three filled, the coffin beside them yields the **Smert Shell** — and its Shell Memory scene plays. (Full ritual detail in the [Smert guide](/shells/smert-the-apostate).)

## Dungeons and the silent gate

Back past the Beacon, the swamp road leaves the village and climbs through the gate: a device jump reaches a meditation point and a 500-coin chest. The button on the stone wall opens the **Waterfall Camp dungeon** — its chest holds **Mammon Coin**. Up the waterfall's left side, the sarcophagi fields yield Gloom and items; the device crossing lands on the bridge beside another meditation point.

The final road runs through a bandit camp — **Common Private Brew**, a 750-coin chest, a rope shot down with ranged attacks — to the **Silent Gate Beacon**. Its descent holds a 1,500-coin chest, and its arena the district boss: **Varigo, Champion of Soligar**. Kill him for the **Voltaic Crown**, cleanse the corruption, deliver the Ova — and the chapter closes.
