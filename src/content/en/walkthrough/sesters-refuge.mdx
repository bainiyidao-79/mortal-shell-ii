The monastery is where Mortal Shell II hides one of its best Shells behind a censer, and one of its best dungeons behind a teleporter. This route runs the courtyard, the interior, and the weapon vault at the bottom.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/walkthrough/sesters-refuge.webp" alt="The halls and courtyards of the monastery" loading="eager" /><figcaption>A censer, a mad monarch, and a weapon sealed behind three tributes.</figcaption></div>

## In through the gate

From Marrow Keep, teleport to the **Outskirts of Mammon**, take the main gate road and turn left. The **teleport portal** here skips the monastery's long approach — though the descent road past it holds NPC Vlas and plenty of **Blood Seeds** for the patient. The far portal lands you in the monastery itself.

Clear the mobs to the great open court of **sisterly afterimages**, and interact with the **censer** at its heart. Inside: the mini-boss **Genessa Seneda** — beat her for the **Nun's Censer**, the item that unlocks the [Genessa Shell](/shells/genessa-the-wayward) when returned to Marrow Keep.

## Up the air currents

Exit through the marked door and ride the **air-current devices** upward to the bridge and its second portal. Crossing unlocks the **Monastery Entrance Beacon**. Cleanse it, then enter the **Fool's Realm**: clear the mobs, pocket the **key** in the corner, open the great door, and drop into the cavern of the **Mad Monarch** mini-boss — his **Monarch's Vestige** drops on death. Collect the Ova and ride back up.

## The nuns' sanctuary

Continue up, clear the courtyard, and enter the **Nuns' Sanctuary**. Bear right, descend the stairs, break the boards to the outside, and meet **Ruk** at the end for **Mammon Map Fragment 1**. The outer gallery descends past mobs to a **trial** — completion pays a **Backplate Stone** — and loops back to the censer court. Head back up to the trial site: an iron door seals a chest that demands **three tributes**, scattered along the marked routes. Pay them all for the **Nightgrasp Stone**.

Back at the Entrance Beacon, rest, then re-enter the sanctuary: stairs up, drop down, into the cavern. **Smash the four glowing signposts** to open the hidden door — behind it, a **Revered Gland** and a **Backplate Stone**. Ride the air current back to the sanctuary, clear it, and open the iron-door chest for the **Devout Stone**.

## The sisters' gate and the Cursed Child

Down the stairs, the **Sisters' Gate Beacon** unlocks after the mob wave. Retrace to the fork and follow it to the end — drop down at the corner into the **Revered Beacon** dungeon, where the [Cursed Child](/weapons/cursed-child) weapon waits behind its beam-tower puzzle.

Ride the elevator back to the Sisters' Gate, cleanse, and return to the court: kill the mobs, ring the bell to raise the road, cross, ring the second ancient bell — and the **Twin Sisters** mini-boss bars the way. Her drops are the **Black Needle** weapon and the **Nun's Badge**. Cleanse the Ova, teleport home to Marrow Keep, and speak with the nun: the **Genessa Shell** is yours.
