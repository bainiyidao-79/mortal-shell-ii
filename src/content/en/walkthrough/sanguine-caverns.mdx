The Sanguine Caverns are Fainweald's longest Fall Gate — a vertical descent through four Beacons, two hide-and-seek boss fights, and a half-dozen Tarstones. Bring ranged ammo and patience; this guide brings the order of operations.

## From the grove to the first gate

Fast travel to the Gloomshade Grove Beacon, follow the right wall down, and take the left passage to **cleanse the corruption** in your path. Clear the area, smash the wooden cart, sweep the supplies, and cross the bridge into the caverns proper — the right-side camp holds **Common Smuggled Wine** and coins.

## The first knight: Lucian

Climb the left slope to a meditation point, drop from the marked ledge, and work up the left passage. The **Bloodcursed Gate Beacon** unlocks on the route — cleanse it, enter, and take the left's first room: squeeze through the crack and loot the **Red Stone** from the cell. The second room's switch opens the door north, and at the corridor's end waits **Lucian, the Hollow Knight**.

**He flees.** Wound him and he escapes through a crack — follow him into the room, where he turns invisible. Kill him properly this time for the **Serpent Stone**. Cleanse the corruption, carry the Ova back to the Beacon, and return to the surface through the shining gate.

## Down the chain

The descent continues past a **Red Stone** on the road and a chest holding the **Deadeye Stone**, then drops into a cave holding a **Synaptic Vessel**. Cross the wooden bridges, and the **Pulsing Cavern Beacon** unlocks. Inside, the left route crosses a bridge to a chest holding the **Blackblood Stone** — cleanse, carry, return.

Drop into the cave ahead, shoot the three eggs on the right with ranged attacks, then break the rock behind them: the chest inside holds the **Mother's Flesh**. Out the cave mouth, hug the cliff, cross two bridges, and the **Scarlet Coast Beacon** unlocks. Its crack hides a **Revered Gland**.

## The maggot and the lost child

The jump device across the coast drops you at the **Maggot-Fiend** boss. Kill it for the **Bile Remnant** stone, drop from the left, and sweep the chest for the **Marsh Pearl** before doubling back for **Nochte's Fang**. Ride the devices back to the coast Beacon, then up the left cave — where **Ruk** waits for a conversation and the **Lost Child** boss gates the crack beyond.

Kill it for the **Lost Clotstone**, and the **Whelp's Feeding Ground Beacon** unlocks. Cleanse it, carry the Ova back, and deliver everything at the Marrow Keep.
