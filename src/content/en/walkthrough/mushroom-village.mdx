Chapter 4 is Mortal Shell II at its most folk-horror: a village of doors that want knocking, statues that want offerings, and one very large illusion at the end. The Axe and Dagger — the game's best early weapon — is also here.

## Into the village, door by door

From the crossing, clear the three mobs on the road and enter through the left gate. Clear the village, then **knock on every door** — the game permits skipping this, but the village remembers politeness. Left of the Mushroom Village Beacon's position, clear the mobs and **offer tribute at the statue**; the second statue waits on a right-side platform ahead, reached by the stairs after the straight road and right turn.

Climb to the door at the top and knock. Descend, turn left and up, and offer at the third statue — the village's chest unlocks. Inside: the **Festive Stone**.

## The Beacon and the chapel key hunt

At the **Mushroom Village Beacon**, cleanse and enter: strike the right door with your weapon, drop, strike the left door, and clear the room — 500 coins and a log inside. Descend the outer stairs, clear the road, and loot the Tarstone chest ahead.

Past the elite and his two mages, cleanse the Beacon's inner chamber and claim the **Ova**. Exit and follow the markers over the bridge, then clear the far side. **Pass the stone door from behind** — only a rear entry yields the **Chapel Key**, looted beneath the statue ahead.

## The Shrine of Trials

Return to the Beacon, enter the left door, and speak with the unlocked **Hunter NPC**: buy the **Cage Key** from him to open the nearby cage and claim the **Ram-Head Totem**. Then use the **Chapel Key** on the **Shrine of Trials** dungeon.

Inside: face the statue and **walk backwards** through the wall; then shove the stone blocks onto their pressure plates room by room. Clear the elite in the chest room, and loot the **Axe and Dagger** from the statue — the game's signature early weapon, with our [full moveset guide](/weapons/axe-and-dagger) covering what it does.

## The gate and the Grand Illusionist

Back at the Beacon, take the right road across the tree-left rope bridge: a 1,000-coin chest, then the **Mushroom Village Gate Beacon**. Cleanse it, clear the road, and reach the stone door — again from behind. Below, **strike the false stone wall** with your weapon and step through into the arena of the **Grand Illusionist Vrannic**. Kill him, take his Tarstone, cleanse the corruption — and the chapter ends with the road to the [Glutted Mire](/walkthrough/glutted-mire) open.
