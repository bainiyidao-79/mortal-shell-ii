Chapter 2 leaves the prologue behind and teaches the game's real economy: Gloom for levels, coins for everything else, and Beacons for staying alive. The Disciple's Grotto is where all three lessons land.

## Ruk, and how money works

Speak with the great crow **Ruk**, then roam: kill the beasts by the puddle to practice. They drop **Gloom** — your XP, spendable only once you reach Marrow Keep — and **coins**, which buy items and upgrade weapons, sidearms and Tarstones. The game is stingier with coins than it looks, so rob every camp twice.

The **silver-gloom tree** on the left yields Gloom when struck. Up the stairs, the **Mether statue** holds a permanent **Common Private Brew**, and the statue grounds demo the ranged-weapon tutorial: strong damage, paid in Resolve.

## The Village Outskirts beacon

Clear the camp up the stairs — a **Heart Stone** hides in its right corner, a readable log sits on the center crates. Break the crates and drop to unlock the **Village Outskirts Beacon**. Cleanse it, enter, open the sarcophagus ahead, and claim the **Muradean Actuator**.

Ride the elevator down, clear the road, and press the wall switch. Cleanse the corruption in the opened gate — and before leaving, note the **Heart Stone** by the fence gate: it only becomes lootable after the cleansing.

## Three fights to the chapter's end

Follow the mountain road and drop at the cliff to the **Lich-Ritualist** boss. He summons suicide-adds; dodge them, punish him. Through the left cave, clear the camp (log on the crates), and take the left passage — the glowing growth yields a **Red Stone**. Clear the bridge, enter the cave ahead, and drop in: the **Disciple's Grotto Beacon** unlocks.

Cleanse it, ride its elevator down, break the left crates, and slip through the gap. Kill the elite, cleanse the corruption, and surface. Left of the Beacon lies **Garrick's Den**: clear it, read the note, return.

Enter the cave before the Beacon for the **Fetid Remains** boss — his **Scorched Doll** drops on death. Sweep, then take the right mountain road down; an elite ambushes the landing, and the rear area holds a **Heart Stone**.

## The Ash Golem and the Ova

Climb to the upper area, speak with Ruk, and push on to the **Ash Golem** boss. His death yields **Watral's Seal**. Sweep the left room (and its note) and the wall-note along the right road, then speak with Ruk one last time. Cleanse the corruption, take the **Ova** — chapter complete, and Marrow Keep awaits.
