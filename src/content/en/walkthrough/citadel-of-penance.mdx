The Citadel of Penance chapter is short, vertical, and extremely rewarding: a Shell behind a warden, a vessel behind a gate, and one of the game's best greatswords behind a prison. Here is the clean route.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/walkthrough/citadel-of-penance.webp" alt="The cells and walls of the Citadel of Penance" loading="eager" /><figcaption>A warden to beat, a body to claim, a blade to liberate.</figcaption></div>

## Into the cells

Teleport to the **One Legged Wolf Beacon** and take the left mountain road up, dropping to the lower platform at the marked spot. Straight ahead, turn left up the stairs, and interact with the **sack on the wall** — the teleport that slips you inside the Citadel of Penance.

Exit the cell and turn right; ride the marked elevator up. At the top, push forward into the **Warden** boss arena.

## The Warden and Eredrim

The Warden's long combination chains are the whole fight: not every strike can be safely blocked, so bring [Tiel's](/shells/tiel-the-acolyte) Shadow Dash pairing and punish the recoveries instead of trading. Kill him — and the **Eredrim Shell** unlocks beside the arena, with its Shell Memory scene.

Use the switch to open the gate and claim the **Synaptic Vessel** inside. Through the door and left, run the passage to its end and ride the device up.

## The walls and the map

Follow the road into the gate; **Ruk** waits on the right — his conversation marks **Fainweald Map Fragment 2** beneath the statue. Open the great door with the lever and push on: the broken bridge's end hides a chest with the **Bulwark Stone**.

Return to the gate and take the left road into the marked cave: the chest inside holds the **Grudge Stone**, with coins on the adjacent wagon. The **Citadel of Penance Beacon** unlocks on the slope — cleanse it, climb the stairs, cross the bridge, and follow the building's right-side stairs upward, then right along the forward road.

## The Martyr's Prison

Back at Ruk's gate, enter the great door again and drop from the left gap. Run the road forward, loot two **Heart Stones**, and read the note on the wall — it points to the **Fortress Gate Lever** you looted in [Gloomshade Grove](/walkthrough/gloomshade-grove). Follow the arrow out of the cave: a meditation point and a Beacon wait at the exit — the **Gloomshade Grove Beacon**, linking the two halves of your map.

Clear the road, open the chest for **Nochte's Fang**, and grab the **Common Smuggled Wine** from the crates. Up the slope, left, and out past the right-side house for another **Common Smuggled Wine** — then into the dungeon ahead. Interact with the object on the table to enter the **Martyr's Prison**: open the cell with your weapon, then the left cell, press the switch inside, and follow the opened gate right. Clear the room, break the crates into the right room — and loot the **Great Martyr's Blade** from its stand. Ride the light home.
