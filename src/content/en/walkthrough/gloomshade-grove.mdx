Gloomshade Grove is Fainweald's western tangle: a trial, two dungeons, a world-boss gate, and one very important NPC whose quest line you must start before finishing the game. Save before the ending, as the guide says — and read on.

## Trial first: the Bombardment Stone

Fast travel to the Gloomshade Grove Beacon and **hug the right-hand wall as you descend.** The trial challenge waits below — win it for the **Bombardment Stone** Tarstone. Then leap from beside the large tree and continue along the route's drop-down.

## The Bloodcursed Lithopod and the Ravaged Hideout

The road leads to the **Bloodcursed Lithopod** world boss. Kill it for the **Fortress Gate Lever**, which opens the derelict hideout dungeon beside the arena:

1. **Break the crate and slip through the gap** into the hideout.
2. **Hug the right wall, clear the chamber.**
3. **Open the chest for Nochte's Fang**, then follow the left-hand road.
4. **Slip through the right-hand wall crack and wait by the corpse** — its ambush dies to patience. Loot the corpse for the **Salvaged Trebuchaxe** sidearm.

## The Conjuror's Hideout: the bell puzzle

Further along the ridge, past the meditation point and the red stone on the rocks, the road reaches the **Conjuror's Hideout** — the grove's puzzle dungeon:

1. **Break the crates and pass the stone door** — attacking the marked wall turns it into a passage.
2. **Climb the steps, break the crate, and shoot the distant bell** with a ranged weapon. A road rises.
3. **Cross, pull the device to its marked position, and pass back through the stone door** — the chest room is now open. Inside: the **Tremor Stone** Tarstone and the **Lavender's Robe** cosmetic.
4. **Two more marked walls** hide in the room's rear — break both for bonus items.

## Before you leave: the Sackhead

The **Sackhead NPC** stands just left of the dungeon's exit. His quest leads to the game's secret ending — and it must be started before you unlock the real one. Our [dedicated guide](/walkthrough/sackhead-secret-ending) covers the full dialogue route.
