Castigator's Keep is the fortress chapter of the Ruins of Mammon: two dungeons, a bone-field boss, a light-lamp gimmick, and a wraith that punishes greed. The [Triarch Repeater](/weapons/triarch-repeater) alone justifies the trip.

## Up the walls

Teleport from Marrow Keep to the **Outskirts of Mammon**, take the main gate road straight, and cross the **Road to Absolution** bridge to its end — the portal there lands you at the keep's gate. Clear the mobs, enter, turn right, and climb the stairs to the wall walk where the **Castigator's Keep Beacon** unlocks.

Cleanse it, clear the mobs, pull the lever to open the great door, and cleanse the Ova. Ride the elevator down — the road below is honest and linear. At the three-mob group, press on into the **Blackwell Cavern** dungeon: fight blind in the dark, use lock-on, and claim the **Triarch Repeater** at its end.

## The Endless Bone-Field

Exit and cross the succession of bridges. At the marked spot, a **hidden wall** breaks for the **Unstable Stone**. At the road's end, the portal crosses to a mob-run road and **Mammon Map Fragment 3**. Ride the air-current device up, clear the mobs, and find the dungeon mouth on the right: the **Whispering Halls**, worth a **Red Stone** sweep.

Leave, take the LEFT of the two entrances ahead, clear the mobs, and exit back toward the great door — which now opens onto the Beacon for rest and resupply. (The castle NPC who doubles any Gloom you tithe him is worth remembering on rich days.)

Take the second entrance now: clear the mobs, ride the elevator up to the **Endless Bone-Field**, and meet the **Hungering Hag**. Her drops are the **Voltaic Essence** and **Voltaic Amber**. Through the crack beyond, the stairs climb to the second floor — map note on the right, and the left **trial** pays the **Thief's Stone**.

## The lamp road and the library

Top of the stairs, through the portal: **destroy the fire-lamps first** — lit, the enemies turn blue-lit and invincible. Clear them, and the **Vestige of Infinity Beacon** unlocks beyond. Cleanse it and enter the **Forbidden Library**: clear the mobs, unlock the mechanism, and the **Wraith-Bound** boss waits at its heart. His kill pays the **Curseblood Stone** — cleanse the Ova and carry everything home.
