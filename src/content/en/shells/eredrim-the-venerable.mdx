Eredrim, the Venerable does not lie in a grave waiting to be claimed — this body is earned the hard way, at the end of a boss fight inside the Citadel of Penance. The good news: the route is short, the arena is fair, and one particular Shell pairing makes the duel dramatically safer.

## Getting there

1. **Make your way to the Citadel of Penance** and fight through to the fortress gate.
2. **After the gate opens, head right** and cleanse the local Beacon first. A saved respawn point next to the arena turns a learning fight into a routine one — dying on the boss doorstep without one is how runs die.
3. **Follow the road forward to the region boss** and engage.

## How to win the fight

The boss mixes long combination attacks, and not every hit in a chain can be blocked safely — trying to tank the full sequence is the classic way to lose half a health bar in one read. Two adjustments fix most of the damage:

- **Bring Tiel, the Acolyte with Shadow Dash.** The pairing's mobility lets you slip through gaps in combinations that a straight block cannot cover, and it repositions you behind the boss for free punishes.
- **Respect the chain, punish the recovery.** Let the combination finish, then cash in your damage during the recovery frames. Greed is what turns a clean fight into a graveyard loop.

Once the boss falls and the short scene plays out, Eredrim, the Venerable is unlocked and added to your rotation.
