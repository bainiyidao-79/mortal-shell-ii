Tiel, the Acolyte is the game's welcome gift to cautious explorers: a full Shell with zero combat attached. You do not need to clear a dungeon, summon anything or beat a boss — you just have to walk to the right graveyard and say yes.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/shells/tiel-the-acolyte.webp" alt="The graveyard where Tiel, the Acolyte rests in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>A shallow grave south-east of the Widow's Overlook Beacon holds one of the game's eight Shells.</figcaption></div>

## Step by step

1. **Travel to the Widow's Overlook Beacon** in the Fainweald region.
2. **Head for the graveyard beside the Beacon.** The route is short and completely safe — nothing on the path needs to be fought, and nothing will ambush you at the grave itself.
3. **Interact with the body in the shallow grave.** The game prompts you to take control of an unknown Shell. Choose **yes**, sit through the short scene, and Tiel, the Acolyte is yours.

That is the entire unlock. If you are collecting Shells in one pass, pair this with [Proxima, the Broodseeker](/shells/proxima-the-broodseeker), who is claimed the same way inside the Blackridge Pass Beacon building.

## Don't leave before the bonus scene

Once Tiel is equipped, return to Marrow Keep and speak with the nun while wearing the Shell. The game triggers a unique, surprisingly warm scene reserved for this combination — one of the few genuinely tender moments in an otherwise grim world. It costs nothing and is easy to miss if you swap Shells before talking to her, so make the detour while Tiel is still on your back.
