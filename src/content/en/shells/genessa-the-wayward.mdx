Genessa, the Wayward — the nun's Shell — is the only body in Mortal Shell II you cannot simply pick up or kill your way to. It is a quest unlock, and the quest item in question is the Sester's Censer: a red censer guarded by a boss near the monastery in the Ruins of Mammon.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/shells/genessa-the-wayward.webp" alt="The red Sester's Censer guarded near the monastery in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The red Sester's Censer is the key to the game's quest-only Shell.</figcaption></div>

## Step 1: reach the censer

1. **Fast travel to the monastery entrance Beacon** in the Ruins of Mammon and follow our [Sester's Refuge beacon route](/beacons/sesters-refuge) inward.
2. **At the marked statue, find the red censer** and interact with it to trigger the boss fight.

## Step 2: win the guardian fight

The censer is not unguarded — defeating the boss bound to it is what frees the item. Keep your composure through its phases, use the arena to reset when pressured, and remember that a cleansed Beacon at the monastery entrance keeps every attempt short.

## Step 3: carry it home

3. **Pick up the Sester's Censer** the boss drops.
4. **Return to Marrow Keep** and hand the censer to the nun herself. A dialogue scene follows — and when it ends, Genessa, the Wayward joins your roster.

## What the Shell does

Genessa arrives with the **Devout Aftershock** ability: summon a duplicate that lands a single weapon attack before vanishing. Used mid-combination, it doubles your pressure at the exact moment enemies expect a breather — a natural fit for aggressive, tempo-heavy play, and a fine partner for fast weapons like the [Axe and Dagger](/weapons/axe-and-dagger).
