Smert, the Apostate swaps the usual boss-door requirement for something stranger: a blood ritual. On a plateau near Prophet's Rest stand three stone sacrifice pools, and the Shell will only acknowledge you once every pool runs red with the blood of enemies killed inside them.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/shells/smert-the-apostate.webp" alt="The sacrifice pools of Prophet's Rest in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Three pools, one requirement: fill them all with blood to claim Smert, the Apostate.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Start from the Citadel of Penance Beacon.** From there, simply follow the road toward the ritual site at Prophet's Rest — no dungeon crawling required on the way.
2. **When you arrive, use the jump pad at Desolate Peak** to cross over to the plateau where the three sacrifice pools stand.
3. **Read the arena before you start.** The pools are spaced across an open platform, with enemies roaming nearby — that is the point, as you are about to turn them into ritual material.

## How the ritual works

The requirement is simple but hands-on: **kill enemies inside the pools** so each one fills with blood. Treat the plateau like a herding exercise:

- Pull enemies toward a pool rather than fighting them where you met them. A blocked swing or a stagger near the rim, followed by a shove of punishment, drops them straight in.
- Work the pools **one at a time**. Splitting attention across three leaves you surrounded in the middle, which is where most deaths on this plateau happen.
- Ranged chip from the shotgun pistol softens targets before they reach the rim, keeping the actual kills predictable.

Once all three pools satisfy the blood requirement, an interaction triggers at the center — and Smert, the Apostate is unlocked, scene and all.
