Sariel, the Endless is the most generous boss in Mortal Shell II — because he keeps coming back, and every rematch pays. One boss line at The Silent Steps yields three rewards: the Sariel Shell, the [Clockwork Scythe](/weapons/clockwork-scythe) primary weapon, and the [Caged Hystrix](/weapons/caged-hystrix) sidearm.

## How the unlock line works

1. **Fast travel to The Silent Steps Beacon** and walk to the large stone platform where the mini-boss waits.
2. **Defeat Sariel the first time.** He will not die properly — he flees through a wall that opens onto the Chamber of Becoming dungeon.
3. **Chase him through the dungeon and fight him again.**
4. **In the final room, destroy the four grave markers** that fuel his resurrections, then finish him for good. The Sariel Shell and Clockwork Scythe unlock on the spot.
5. **Before leaving, climb the stairs at the back of the room** — the Caged Hystrix sidearm sits near the dungeon exit.

## Fight notes: respect the reach

Sariel wields a scythe with absurd horizontal range, and most of his damage comes from sweeping arcs that punish frontal positioning. Two habits carry the whole line:

- **Never stand in front.** Circle to his sides and back; his swings cover a wide arc but his tracking is poor once committed.
- **Save healing for the grave-marker room.** The final phase pairs the boss with four objects to break, and entering it wounded turns a routine finish into a loop.
