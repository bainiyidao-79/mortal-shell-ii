Like Tiel before it, Proxima, the Broodseeker is a claim-it-yourself Shell: the body rests inside the very building that houses the Blackridge Pass Beacon, and claiming it requires nothing sharper than the interact button.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/shells/proxima-the-broodseeker.webp" alt="The beacon building hiding Proxima, the Broodseeker in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The Blackridge Pass Beacon building doubles as Proxima's resting place.</figcaption></div>

## How to claim it

1. **Fast travel to the Blackridge Pass Beacon** in the Fainweald region.
2. **Step inside the building that contains the Beacon.** You will not have to fight anything on the way in.
3. **Interact with the Shell resting inside**, choose **yes** when the game asks you to take control of an unknown Shell, and watch the brief scene. Proxima is now yours.

Because the Beacon and the Shell share a room, this is the natural first stop of any Shell sweep: cleanse the Beacon, claim the body, and fast travel out without a single swing thrown.

## Why bother with Proxima

Proxima is one of the eight collectible Shells, and Shells are more than costumes — each carries its own health, stamina and a signature ability that changes how you approach fights. Grabbing the two free ones early (Tiel and Proxima) gives you options before you commit to the boss-gated bodies like [Eredrim, the Venerable](/shells/eredrim-the-venerable) or the ritual-bound [Smert, the Apostate](/shells/smert-the-apostate).
