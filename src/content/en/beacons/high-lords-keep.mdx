The High Lord's Keep Beacon is the doorstep of the Royal Crypt of Mammon — which makes it the respawn point for one of the game's key Shell fights. It also holds the sixth and final Mammon Map Fragment.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/high-lords-keep.webp" alt="The High Lord's Keep Beacon in the Ruins of Mammon" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The crypt's doorstep — cleanse it before you challenge the royal dead.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Start at the Gate of Mammon Beacon** and follow the road toward the High Lord's Keep.
2. **Cleanse the High Lord's Keep Beacon.**
3. **Collect Mammon Map Fragment 6 right beside the Beacon** — the last of the region's six fragments.

## What it stages

The keep's whole purpose is what lies beneath: the [Royal Crypt of Mammon](/shells/lazlo-the-justicar), its mini-boss, and the Lazlo Shell claimed in the chamber behind the fight. With the Beacon cleansed, attempts run from the crypt's front step — which turns a punishing walk into a five-second reload.
