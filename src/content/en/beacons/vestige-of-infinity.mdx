The Vestige of Infinity Beacon stands at the edge of the Ruins of Mammon's map — the last overworld checkpoint before the region's vertical descent into the Seat of Infinity and its bone-strewn depths.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/vestige-of-infinity.webp" alt="The Vestige of Infinity Beacon at the edge of the Ruins of Mammon" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The map's edge, the region's last Beacon, and the road down.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Travel from the Castigator's Keep Beacon** and continue along the forward road.
2. **Collect Mammon Map Fragment 2** along the way if you have not already swept the fortress loop.
3. **Arrive at the Vestige of Infinity and cleanse the Beacon.**

## What it stages

The Vestige is the launch point for the spire's interior: the ascent through the bone-field where a hungry matriarch waits, the forbidden library dungeon, and the Wraith-swept halls below — all detailed in the [Castigator's Keep region walkthrough](/walkthrough/castigators-keep). With the Beacon cleansed, every one of those attempts starts at the door instead of across the region.
