The One Legged Wolf Beacon marks the tavern district — the liveliest corner of Fainweald and home to a Shell quest, a free sidearm and two hidden Synaptic Vessels.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/one-legged-wolf.webp" alt="The One Legged Wolf Beacon and tavern road in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Beacon, fragment, tavern — the district's whole checklist in one trip.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Fast travel back to the Mushroom Village Gate Beacon** and follow the road to the One Legged Wolf position.
2. **Cleanse the One Legged Wolf Beacon.**
3. **Collect Fainweald Map Fragment 5 nearby** — it sits close to the Beacon along the district road.

## What the district holds

With the Beacon cleansed, everything the tavern offers becomes cheap to attempt:

- The [Bard's Lute](/weapons/bards-lute) — a free stage pickup inside the tavern.
- The **Gragu quest** — help the drunkard find the Heart of Vatra for the game's hidden eighth Shell.
- Two [Synaptic Vessels](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-one-legged-wolf-1), one on a tablet and one behind a hidden floor.
- The tavern's shop counter and its handful of conversation scenes.
