The Silent Steps is where the Ruins of Mammon's overworld ends — and where one of the game's best reward lines begins. The Beacon sits at the region's far edge, a stone's throw from the platform where Sariel, the Endless waits.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/the-silent-steps.webp" alt="The Silent Steps Beacon at the edge of the Ruins of Mammon" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The last Beacon of Mammon — and the first step of the Sariel line.</figcaption></div>

## How to reach it

1. **Start at the Vestige of Infinity Beacon** — the region's previous checkpoint.
2. **Run the road to the Seat of Infinity junction, and take the right-hand path** at the bone-field.
3. **Cleanse The Silent Steps Beacon** at the route's end.

## What it stages

This Beacon is the rest point for the Sariel boss line: the first fight on the stone platform south-east, the chase into the [Chamber of Becoming](/weapons/clockwork-scythe), and the grave-marker finale that yields the Sariel Shell, the [Clockwork Scythe](/weapons/clockwork-scythe) and the [Caged Hystrix](/weapons/caged-hystrix). It also anchors the roads to the [Forgotten Tower](/weapons/axatana), where the Axatana waits — making this single checkpoint the most weapon-dense rest point in the region.
