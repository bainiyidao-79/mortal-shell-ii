The Blackridge Pass Beacon sits inside its own building on the cliffs of Fainweald — which makes it a two-for-one stop, since the [Proxima, the Broodseeker](/shells/proxima-the-broodseeker) Shell rests in the same room.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/blackridge-pass.webp" alt="The Blackridge Pass Beacon building in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Cleanse the Beacon, claim the Shell, take the fragment — one stop, three rewards.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Travel back to the Widow's Overlook Beacon** and set out for Blackridge Pass to the north.
2. **Cleanse the Blackridge Pass Beacon** inside its building.
3. **With the Beacon lit, follow the route to collect Fainweald Map Fragment 3** — it hangs on the natural path a short walk away.
4. **Return to the Beacon spawn point, turn your view, and cross the bridge in the direction shown** to link this checkpoint into the wider road network.

## What this checkpoint unlocks

Blackridge Pass is the staging point for the cliff-side layer of Fainweald: the road down into the [Flooded Village dungeon](/weapons/forgotten-crossbow), the [Synaptic Vessel](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-blackridge-pass) guarded by its pit boss, and the eventual route around to [Gloomshade Grove](/beacons/gloomshade-grove). Every one of those trips starts cheaper with this Beacon cleansed.
