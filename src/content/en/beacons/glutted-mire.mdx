The Glutted Mire — the northeast Fall Gate of Fainweald — holds three Beacons, and the third changes how you travel the entire overworld. This route cleanses them in the order the swamp actually allows.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/glutted-mire.webp" alt="The swamp trails of the Glutted Mire in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Three beacons, one boss, and a fast-travel network as the prize.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Start at the Mushroom Village Gate Beacon** and head for the mire.
2. **Cleanse the Seeping Mire Beacon** first — it is the swamp's front door.
3. **From the Seeping Mire, take the road on the Beacon's left** toward the village.
4. **Cleanse the Sunken Village Beacon.** While you are there, grab the [Revered Gland beside the stairs](/collectibles/revered-glands-sunken-village).
5. **Continue on the left-hand road toward Magdalena's Glade.** The final Beacon only unlocks after the **Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods** boss fight.

## The payoff: Mether's Breath

Defeating Magdalena awards the **Mether's Breath** ability — the power to cleanse corruption walls across the overworld, which is the gate-keeping mechanic for half the region beacons in Fainweald and beyond. If you have been bumping into translucent corruption barriers, this fight is the key.
