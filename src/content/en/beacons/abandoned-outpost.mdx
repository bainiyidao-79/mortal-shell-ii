The Ruins of Mammon — the game's second overworld region — opens through two linked Beacons: the Abandoned Outpost and the Gate of Mammon. This route cleanses both and picks up two Map Fragments on the way.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/abandoned-outpost.webp" alt="The Abandoned Outpost Beacon area in the Ruins of Mammon" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Outpost first, gate second, two fragments on the road between.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Start at the Outskirts of Mammon Beacon** and follow the video-route line out.
2. **Collect Mammon Map Fragment 5** along the approach.
3. **Return toward the Outskirts and push on to the Abandoned Outpost. Cleanse its Beacon.**
4. **From the Outpost, run the road to the Gate of Mammon and cleanse that Beacon** — the district's anchor for everything east, including the [Ballistazooka's dungeon](/weapons/ballistazooka).
5. **Return to the Outpost road once more and follow it to collect Mammon Map Fragment 4.**

## Shop note: the Collector

South of the Abandoned Outpost Beacon, a night-only vendor — **the Collector** — sells rare wares, including infusions. If you are sweeping the Outpost at all, wait for nightfall and check his stock before moving on; some of his catalog is otherwise easy to miss entirely.
