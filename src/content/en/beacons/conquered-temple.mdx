The Conquered Temple is the southeast Fall Gate — a conquered cathedral region entered through the monastery's Sisters' Gate. Three Beacons mark its route, and every broken bridge along the way has the same solution: ring the bell.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/conquered-temple.webp" alt="The conquered cathedral region of Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Three beacons, one conqueror, and bells that rebuild the roads.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Enter through the purification door beside the Sisters' Gate Beacon** ([Sester's Refuge route](/beacons/sesters-refuge)) to reach the conquered temple region.
2. **Head for the winter lake and cleanse its Beacon** — the district's front checkpoint.
3. **From the lake, push to the prophet's chamber and cleanse that Beacon.**
4. **Fight on to the Conqueror boss and defeat him.** The final Beacon — the conqueror's tide — unlocks with his fall.
5. **Cleanse the tide Beacon** to complete the district. The lake bridges you broke or bypassed can be repaired by **striking their bells**, which reopens permanent shortcuts across the region.

## District notes

The region doubles as a collectible corridor: the road into the [Valley of the Revered](/walkthrough/valley-of-the-revered) carries martyrs' ashes, heart stones and more, and the cathedral's three-door puzzle hides an infamy reward for thorough players. The Conqueror himself is famously manageable with a summon-heavy build — Genessa's duplicate makes the arena noticeably kinder.
