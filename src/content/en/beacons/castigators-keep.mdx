The Castigator's Keep Beacon anchors the fortress district of the Ruins of Mammon — and its roads are the richest Map Fragment corridor in the region, with two fragments on one short loop.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/castigators-keep.webp" alt="The Castigator's Keep Beacon and fortress roads" loading="eager" /><figcaption>One Beacon, two fragments, one road east to the spire.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Start at the Outskirts of Mammon Beacon** and follow the road to the fortress.
2. **Cleanse the Castigator's Keep Beacon.**
3. **Follow the fortress roads to collect Mammon Map Fragment 3** on the marked line.
4. **Continue the loop to pick up Mammon Map Fragment 2.**
5. **Push east along the road toward the Vestige of Infinity** — the next Beacon in the region chain — [covered in its own guide](/beacons/vestige-of-infinity).

## What this district stages

The keep is your staging point for the fortress's dungeon layer — including the cavern that hides the [Triarch Repeater](/weapons/triarch-repeater) — and for the eastern roads toward the spire. Its Beacon also sits close to a hidden wall section hiding a bonus Tarstone for observant players.
