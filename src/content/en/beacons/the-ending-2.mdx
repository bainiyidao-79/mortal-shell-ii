With the first branch severed, the endgame region resets you at its corrupted Beacon — and opens the right-hand road for the second arc of the chain. This leg holds two Beacons and the fight with Oralech.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/the-ending-2.webp" alt="The audience bridge of the endgame region in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Two-phase guardian, rising platform, second branch.</figcaption></div>

## The second arc

1. **From the corrupted Beacon, take the right-hand road** this time.
2. **Arrive at the audience bridge and cleanse its Beacon** — the arc's first checkpoint.
3. **Enter the arena and fight Oralech.** Two phases: the first is the figure you may remember from the prologue; the second, the Reforged form, hits harder but moves the same. Genessa's summoned duplicate trivializes much of the pressure.
4. **Win, claim the fallen head, and ride the rising platform** to the upper level.
5. **Cleanse the hollow cradle Beacon**, clear its shrine of the Ova, and **sever the second branch** of the red monolith.

The region teleports you back to the corrupted Beacon once more — one branch remains.
