The Prisoner's Domain is Fainweald's northwest Fall Gate — a vertical prison district where four Beacons mark the descent. Cleanse them in order and the whole gate becomes a fast-travel loop; skip one and every death in the lower levels costs a full climb.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/prisoners-domain.webp" alt="The descending prison paths of the Prisoner's Domain" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Four checkpoints down the prison's spine — cleanse them top to bottom.</figcaption></div>

## The cleansing order

1. **Enter through the silent gate** from the [Ruins of Nochte](/beacons/ruins-of-nochte) road — the district's front Beacon stands just inside.
2. **Cleanse the first three checkpoints in sequence as the main road descends:** the gate Beacon, the gallows-road checkpoint, and the executioner's pulpit — the last reached by climbing the stairs beyond the gallows.
3. **Return to the gate area once the descent road forks,** and take the side path to the drain-culvert dungeon route — its exit opens onto the domain's final Beacon, the deepest checkpoint.
4. **Cleanse the final Beacon.** From here, the [boss gauntlet of the domain](/walkthrough/prisoners-domain) — including the nameless prisoner at the bottom — starts from a rest point, not a climb.

## Route discipline

The district's roads double back on themselves constantly. The rule that saves runs: **cleanse on sight, never on the way back.** Every Beacon you pass unlit is a future corpse run.
