The Ruins of Nochte Beacon sits on the western rim of Fainweald — a checkpoint with two jobs: staging the village fights around it, and opening the road to the Prisoner's Domain gate.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/ruins-of-nochte.webp" alt="The Ruins of Nochte Beacon area in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Cleanse the Beacon, pocket Fragment 1, face the silent gate.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Travel from the Widow's Overlook Beacon** and run the overworld road toward Nochte.
2. **Cleanse the Ruins of Nochte Beacon**, then **collect Fainweald Map Fragment 1** at its feet.
3. **Push on along the road to the silent gate** — the entrance to the [Prisoner's Domain](/beacons/prisoners-domain), the region's northwest Fall Gate.

## Notes for the district

The village around the Beacon holds its own small rewards — an early [Smert ritual site](/shells/smert-the-apostate) is reachable from here via the blood pools plateau, and the local dungeon hides coins and consumables worth sweeping. Treat the Beacon as your western hub: everything from graveyard Shells to gate pushes radiates from this point.
