Below Marrow Keep lies the game's final region — an unmapped endgame expanse with no map of its own, gated behind a corrupted Beacon and three pulsing branches of a red monolith. This guide covers the first arc of its Beacon chain.

## Entry requirements

The final road opens from the Marrow Keep Beacon's ovum altar once you have pushed both overworld regions to roughly 95% Beacon purification and delivered the stolen Ova. When the cutscene plays and the floor opens, the endgame has begun.

## The first arc: Forgetting Chamber → Audience Hall → Believers' Sanctuary

1. **Drop into the underground road from Marrow Keep** and ride the first portal across.
2. **Clear to the corrupted Beacon.** It will not light until you sever all three branches of the red monolith — this first trip is about scouting, not completing.
3. **Take the right-hand road first:** follow the lower path, clear the portal crossing, and climb the stairs to **cleanse the first endgame Beacon** — the elevated bridge checkpoint.
4. **Push forward into the barren shrine arena** beyond it, clear the pit, deliver the Ova, and return.
5. **Cross the bridge and defeat the two-phase guardian** — its second form is famously manageable with Genessa's summon. The platform rises to reveal the **hollow cradle Beacon** — cleanse it, clear its shrine, and sever the first branch of the monolith.

Severing a branch teleports you back to the corrupted Beacon — each severed branch is a third of the key to lighting it.
