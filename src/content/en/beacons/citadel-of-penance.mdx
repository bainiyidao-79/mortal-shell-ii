The Citadel of Penance does not roll out a carpet. Its Beacon sits deep inside the fortress, and the practical way in is not the front door — it is the teleport sack hidden in the scene.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/citadel-of-penance.webp" alt="Inside the Citadel of Penance in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Through the sack, into the fortress, straight to the Beacon.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Find the teleport sack in the scene and interact with it** — it zips you directly inside the Citadel of Penance.
2. **Run the interior route to the Citadel of Penance Beacon and cleanse it.**
3. **Collect Fainweald Map Fragment 2** along the route, close to the Beacon.

## What this Beacon stages

The Citadel is a boss district: the warden-style region boss deeper inside drops the [Eredrim, the Venerable](/shells/eredrim-the-venerable) Shell. With the Beacon cleansed, every attempt at that fight starts from inside the walls instead of outside the gate — the difference between a learning loop and a slog.
