The Sanguine Caverns — Fainweald's north Fall Gate — string four Beacons along one descending chain. The district is built as a relay: each checkpoint stages the next, and the final one stages the gate's boss.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/sanguine-caverns.webp" alt="The red-lit passages of the Sanguine Caverns" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Four Beacons down one bloody chain — cleanse them in order.</figcaption></div>

## The chain, in order

1. **From the Gloomshade Grove gate road, cleanse the bloodcursed gate Beacon** — the caverns' front door. The [final Synaptic Vessel](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-bloodcursed-gate) sits on the marked road beyond it.
2. **Descend to the second checkpoint** — the pulsing cavern Beacon — where the district's dungeon loop begins.
3. **Continue the descent to the scarlet coast Beacon,** the staging point for the shoreline fights.
4. **Cleanse the final Beacon at the feeding grounds.** It is the rest point before the gate's closing boss and the Ova delivery that ends the district.

## Why the order matters

The caverns' geometry funnels you downward with few shortcuts. A missed Beacon means a full re-descent from the gate — and the district's ranged enemies make that climb painful. Treat the chain as a rule: no Beacon left behind on the way down.
