The monastery — known in the world's own tongue as Sester's Refuge — is the haunted convent district of the Ruins of Mammon, and the source of the game's most important quest item. Two Beacons mark its progress: the Entrance and the Sisters' Gate.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/sesters-refuge.webp" alt="The monastery beacon route in the Ruins of Mammon" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Two beacons, one censer, one Shell waiting back at Marrow Keep.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Start at the Outskirts of Mammon Beacon** and run the road toward the monastery, passing the tall tower position where **Mammon Map Fragment 1** sits — grab it.
2. **Arrive at the monastery and cleanse the Entrance Beacon.**
3. **From the Entrance, follow the route onward and cleanse the Sisters' Gate Beacon** — the district's deeper checkpoint.

## Why this district matters: the censer

The monastery's statue courtyard hides the **red Sester's Censer**, and touching it triggers a boss fight. Win, and the censer becomes a carried quest item: deliver it to the nun in Marrow Keep to unlock [Genessa, the Wayward](/shells/genessa-the-wayward) — the game's only quest-gated Shell.

The district's interior also feeds the [Valley of the Revered](/walkthrough/valley-of-the-revered) road — the conquered temple region beyond the Sisters' Gate — so both Beacons earn their keep twice.
