Two branches severed. The road back to the corrupted Beacon now opens the left-hand cave — the Forgetting Chamber's deeper half — for the third and last arc before the endgame's heart.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/beacons/the-ending-3.webp" alt="The obsidian vault of the endgame region" loading="eager" /><figcaption>One court, one vault, one brutal monolith between you and the final branch.</figcaption></div>

## The third arc

1. **Return to the Forgetting Chamber cave and exit to the right**, riding the air-current device to the upper platform and its portal crossing.
2. **Run the passage to the wide court and cleanse the monks' court Beacon** on its right side.
3. **Clear its shrine** of enemies and deliver the Ova as before.
4. **Continue the linear road** — there are no forks here — through the root-tangled hall to the **obsidian vault Beacon. Cleanse it**, trigger the great door, and clear the Residue shrine.
5. **Fight the Monolith.** The game's most infamous stat-check: enormous health, enormous damage, and an omnidirectional laser phase. Bring your strongest comfort build — heavy sustain like the Unwieldly Stone's lifesteal or a dodge-first loadout — and win through attrition, not flash.
6. **Sever the third branch.** The corrupted Beacon is now fully unsealed.
