Mortal Shell II released on August 20, 2026, and the review picture is unusually consistent: critics agree the sequel's transformation from compact soulslike into a large open-world action RPG mostly lands. Below is the score snapshot, what reviewers actually said, and a realistic read on how much time the campaign will take from you.

## The scores at a glance

- **IGN: 8/10**, calling the pivot to a large-scale action RPG a success on the whole.
- **Metacritic: 83 (PC)** and **88 (PS5)** at launch window.

## What reviewers praised

IGN's verdict highlights the sequel's confident expansion: the new ideas are strong, and the addition of active Shell abilities enriches builds far beyond the first game's moveset-swapping. The open world earns special mention for quietly steering players toward its hidden content instead of relying on map clutter, and the expanded combat system gives each Shell a genuinely distinct personality in a way the original only hinted at. The dark-fantasy art direction, long a series strength, carries over intact.

## Where critics dinged it

The complaints recur across outlets: assorted technical rough edges, a world that is easy to get lost in when you stray off the critical path, and late-game challenges that can be skipped or trivialised once you know the systems. None of these overturned the core verdict — the scale-up worked.

## How long is Mortal Shell II?

The development team has shared official expectations, and they are generous by soulslike standards:

- **Story-focused run: roughly 30 hours** — about double the length of the original game's campaign.
- **Standard completion with moderate side exploration: 30–40 hours.**
- **Full completion: 60+ hours observed**, including repeated boss challenges, full map clearing, and multi-run content.

The extra length comes from the larger connected world, more dungeons, and a much deeper weapon and Shell system. The team also notes they tuned the map structure specifically to reduce aimless backtracking between regions.

## After the credits: NG+ and Night Mode

Two systems extend the game past the ending. **New Game+** carries your progress into a harder remixed run, while **Night Mode** raises combat difficulty substantially in exchange for higher-tier rewards — a deliberate answer to the "what now?" problem after a first completion. If you are deciding whether the campaign justifies the price, the honest summary is this: the story run alone is solid value at 30 hours, and completionists will get two to three times that.
