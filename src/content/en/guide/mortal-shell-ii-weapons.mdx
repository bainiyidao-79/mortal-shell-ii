## Mortal Shell II weapons at a glance

Mortal Shell II has **eight melee weapons**, each with its own moveset, its own pool of **tar stones** (equipable stones that add active and passive effects), one or more weapon-specific **infusions** (permanent elemental/status effects), and a unique **ability stone** usually taken from a gate boss.

This guide is based on **FightinCowboy's complete weapon guide** ("Mortal Shell 2 - Complete Weapon Guide: Movesets, Infusions, & Locations", 116K+ views), which covers every weapon in depth. The short version of his core advice:

- **Curse** (via the Arbiter's Prize or Curse Blood Stone) is arguably the best status in the game — cursed enemies that try to hit you get staggered and damage themselves, like a reflective iron maiden.
- **Duality Stone** effectively doubles your DPS on the two dual-wield weapons.
- **Stasis** (on the Obsidian Hammer and Axe Tana) is so oppressive it will likely get nerfed — it slows enemies until they can't fight back.

## The eight weapons

### 1. Aconclast (starter)

The default weapon you begin with. Don't sleep on it — it's one of only two weapons with access to **Curse**. Recommended setup from the guide: **Zealot Stone** for the light-combo finisher (a 150% resolve gain and a one-two combo finisher), **Arbiter's Prize** infusion for Blood Curse, and the **Strike Stone** ability for a flashy combo with good break and AoE damage. Light attacks chain into a one-two, heavies thrust into a heavy overhead, the running light is a jump, and the running heavy is a golf swing.

### 2. Axe & Dagger

Likely the first weapon you unlock. Its best-in-slot support is the **Duality Stone**, which turns every hit in the base combo into a strike-twice attack — one-two-one-two — effectively **doubling DPS**. It's one of only two dual-wield weapons that can use it. Pair with the **Arbiter's Prize** blood-curse infusion. To get it: from the mushroom village, cross the bridge, find the **illusory archway**, walk through the statue behind it to grab a key, then clear the **Shrine of Trials**.

### 3. Veteran's Battle Axe

An early, fantastic heavy weapon. The guide recommends the **Thief Stone** (warp stacks speed up your swings) over the combo finishers, which slow the moves down. It's the only weapon with access to a **permanent burn infusion**, which also looks great on it. To get it: head north through **Black Ridge Pass** to the **King's Crypt** — a trap-filled dungeon (floor spikes, wall arrows) that rewards you with the axe at the end. The running heavy attack is a standout: a decimating strike the guide calls "absurd" good for creating distance.

### 4. Great Martyr's Blade

The greatsword, and the **only weapon with access to Frost** — the infusion that makes it viable, since it's otherwise one of the slowest weapons in the game. Recommended: **Captive's Scabstone** ability (a 300% strike with massive stagger, from a gate boss), and after unlocking the **Warden Stone**, the weapon can freeze enemies outright. To get it: go west through **Gloomshade Grove**, kill the boss summoned by striking the stone (the blood-cursed lithopod), then take the key into the **martyr's prison**. The frost gate itself is in the far southeast of the world — a second-half-of-the-game trek.

### 5. Obsidian Hammer

Found mid-to-late game. Supports the **Cleric Stone** for lockdown plus big crits, and has access to both **Trauma** (stagger build-up over time) and **Stasis** — one of only two weapons that can use stasis, which per the guide is "just as broken as curse." The unique **Colossus Stone** ability adds shockwave AoE. To get it: from the outskirts of **Mammon**, go southwest into the **Obsidianite mines**, a hectic mining area full of pickaxe-wielders and rock-throwers.

### 6. Axe Tana

The guide's personal favorite — a fast axe with the best of both worlds: **Duality Stone** support (it's the other dual-wield weapon) and access to **Stasis** via the **Hexopod Core** ability despite its already-fast attack speed. Note the Hexopod Core needs leveling for stagger immunity before it's worth the risk. To get it: head south and use one of the teleportation gates to reach a small island tower — there's an enemy wielding it, and you can pick the weapon up right next to her. No boss gate, just get there.

### 7. Black Needle

A spear, and one of the latest weapons. Its unique infusion is **Phantom**, which builds up damage and then bursts out in a large stagger splash — strictly exclusive to the spear. The **Conqueror's Reward** ability adds big heavy damage. To get it: clear **Cester's gate** in the far south — the full spear package (weapon, a ranged option, and the Phantom stone) comes from that single area.

### 8. Clockwork Scythe

The fan favorite. Its signature is **chainsaw mode**: the **Scholar's Wormstone** ability consumes resolve over time to set the scythe's blade spinning, adding 35% weapon-damage multi-hits to every attack — the guide compares it to Monster Hunter's savage axe. The alternative **Clockwork Shardstone** is, per the guide, "quite literally just going to be chainsaw mode" in a more literal sense. Pair with the **Arbiter's Prize** blood curse for the thematically perfect bloody scythe. To get it: clear the **Chamber of Becoming**, reached from the outskirts of Mammon.

## Systems every weapon shares

- **Tar stones** are your equipment slots: Zealot, Thief, Duality, Grudge, Parasitic, Cleric, Warden, Strike, and more. They gain tiers the more you use them (as covered in our [gameplay overview](/guide/mortal-shell-ii-gameplay)).
- **Infusions** are permanent per-weapon effects: Curse, Burn, Frost, Stasis, Trauma, Phantom, Poison, and Voltaic.
- **Ability stones** like Magdalena's Momento, Captive's Scabstone, Conqueror's Reward, Lost Clot Stone, Colossus Stone, and Hexopod Core mostly come from **gate bosses** — the bosses at the end of each world section — so it's worth clearing gates even when you don't need the weapon behind them.

## Which weapon should you pick first?

Per the guide's recommendations: keep the **Aconclast** for early Curse value, grab the **Axe & Dagger** as soon as you can handle the Shrine of Trials, and beeline the **Veteran's Battle Axe** in King's Crypt if you like heavies. Once Frost is available, the **Great Martyr's Blade** goes from slow to broken — and if you want the community's late-game monster, the **Clockwork Scythe** with chainsaw mode is waiting in the Chamber of Becoming.
