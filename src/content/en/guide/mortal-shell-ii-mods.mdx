## Does Mortal Shell II have mods?

The game launched on August 20, 2026, and its modding scene is still **young**. What's confirmed so far:

- **Nexus Mods has an official game hub** for Mortal Shell II (next.nexusmods.com/games/mortalshell2), which is the standard home for the game's future mods. A dedicated hub page means the site's tooling — mod listing, version tracking, Vortex support — is ready for uploads as they come.
- **The community is already talking about it.** Threads on r/MortalShell (like "Mortal Shell 2 mods") show players actively asking for and discussing mod potential — texture and visual tweaks, difficulty options, and quality-of-life changes are the usual early requests for this genre.

## What kinds of mods to expect

Based on how the first *Mortal Shell* scene developed, early modding for Mortal Shell II will likely center on:

- **Visual and shader tweaks** — the game's heavy-metal-meets-H.R.-Giger art direction invites lighting and contrast adjustments
- **Camera and field-of-view options** — a common early request for action RPGs
- **Save and progression tools** — given the game's currency-loss-on-death loop, backup/edit tools tend to appear early
- **Difficulty modifiers** — pairing with or replacing the [trainer](/guide/mortal-shell-ii-trainer) options for players who want fine-grained control
