## Are there trainers for Mortal Shell II?

Yes. Mortal Shell II is a **single-player** action RPG, and PC trainers for it already exist from two established cheat-tool makers: **FLiNG** and **WeMod**.

### FLiNG Trainer

FLiNG maintains a dedicated Mortal Shell II trainer page (flingtrainer.com) advertising a **trainer with 42 cheats** — one of the larger option sets you'll find for the game. FLiNG trainers are standalone downloadable tools for the PC (Steam) version.

### WeMod

WeMod's community forums carry an official **"Mortal Shell II Cheats and Trainer for Steam"** thread, meaning WeMod supports the game in its trainer app. WeMod works by selecting the game in the client and toggling cheats with hotkeys.

## Things to know before using a trainer

- **Trainers are for offline, single-player use.** Mortal Shell II has no co-op or PvP where a trainer would affect other players, but modifying game memory can still trip store or save-integrity edge cases — use them on saves you don't mind risking.
- **Version matching matters.** Trainers are built for specific game versions; after a game update, a trainer may need a matching update before its cheats work. Check the trainer page's supported-version notes before downloading.
- **Keep your saves backed up.** Standard advice for any trainer use: copy your save folder before toggling cheats, since some options (like one-shot kills or currency multipliers) can soft-lock progression in a soulslike where dying and losing currency is part of the loop.

## A fair warning from the game's own design

Part of what makes Mortal Shell II interesting is its economy of risk — the shell system that ejects you at zero health, currency you drop on death, and tar stones that level up through use. Trainers that remove those pressures change the feel of the game considerably.
