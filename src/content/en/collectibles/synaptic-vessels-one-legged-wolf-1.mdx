The first of the two One-Legged Wolf vessels is a short walk with one small jump in the middle. Synaptic Vessels permanently raise how much each Mether's Pulse heals once consumed at a Beacon, so this two-minute detour pays out for the rest of the run.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-one-legged-wolf-1.webp" alt="The stone tablet holding a Synaptic Vessel near the One-Legged Wolf" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Follow the slope, cross the pillars, loot the tablet.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Leave the One Legged Wolf Beacon down the slope** and cross the bridge.
2. **Keep straight until you see two pillars standing together** — jump through the gap between them to the far side.
3. **Continue straight to the stone doorway.** The **stone tablet stands to its right.**
4. **Loot the Synaptic Vessel from the tablet.**

Spend it at the next Beacon you touch — an unconsumed vessel is dead weight.

One vessel not enough? The [second One-Legged Wolf vessel](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-one-legged-wolf-2) hides underground behind the same tavern, through a hidden transparent floor.
