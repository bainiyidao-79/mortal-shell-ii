Synaptic Vessels are the other half of Mortal Shell II's healing economy. Where [Revered Glands](/collectibles/revered-glands-marrow-keep) add flask charges, Synaptic Vessels make each charge heal **more** — consumed at any Beacon, each one permanently raises the amount a single Mether's Pulse restores.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-marrow-keep.webp" alt="The merchant stall selling Synaptic Vessels in Marrow Keep" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The Marrow Keep merchant sells a Synaptic Vessel alongside his other wares.</figcaption></div>

## How the two upgrades stack

The optimal order recommended by survivors of the early bosses:

1. **Glands first.** Extra charges give you the safety net to learn fights — take the three early Revered Glands before anything else.
2. **Vessels second.** Once you stop running dry mid-fight, raising the value of every heal turns three-fight runs into five-fight runs.

And remember: **vessels in your inventory do nothing.** Spend them at a Beacon the moment you pick them up.

## The Marrow Keep vessel

The simplest Synaptic Vessel requires no dungeon at all:

1. **From the Marrow Keep Beacon, take the left stairs** toward the **Gloom Siphon**.
2. **Turn right at the Siphon** to find the merchant's stall.
3. **Buy the Synaptic Vessel from his list.** Coins spent on this rarely regret themselves.

The other vessels hide in the world — [Blackridge Pass](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-blackridge-pass), two around the [One-Legged Wolf](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-one-legged-wolf-1), and one past the [gate toward the Sanguine Caverns](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-bloodcursed-gate).
