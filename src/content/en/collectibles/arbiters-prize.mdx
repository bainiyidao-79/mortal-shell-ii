The Arbiter's Prize is the Tarstone that turns a weapon into a curse. Spend 30 Resolve and your blade drinks blood — every strike carries the affliction until the infusion's strike count runs out. It drops straight from a field mini-boss below the Widow's Overlook Beacon, no trial required.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/arbiters-prize.webp" alt="The field mini-boss guarding the Arbiter's Prize" loading="eager" /><figcaption>One field boss, one permanent weapon upgrade — if you can take it from him.</figcaption></div>

## How to get it

1. **Fast travel to the Widow's Overlook Beacon.**
2. **Turn away from the Beacon and take the downhill road.** The field mini-boss waits along it.
3. **Kill the mini-boss.** The Arbiter's Prize drops on the spot — no trial tablet involved.

## What it does

- **Cost: 30 Resolve** to activate the infusion.
- **Level 1:** the curse empowers **4 strikes** before fading.
- **Upgrades extend the count**, and the final level makes the infusion **permanent** — every swing carries the curse for as long as Resolve holds.

The arithmetic is simple: four cursed swings per activation is already a big damage window; a permanent one redefines the weapon. Infusion Tarstones like this are why Resolve management matters — the more you attack, the more Resolve you generate, the more curse you can afford.

## Who wants it

Aggressive builds that stay in melee range get the most from the Prize — especially fast weapons that spend the strike window quickly. It is thematically mandated equipment for the [Clockwork Scythe](/weapons/clockwork-scythe), and honestly, who are we to argue with a bloody scythe.
