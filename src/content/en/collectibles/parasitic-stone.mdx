The Parasitic Stone is combat Tarstone with one appetite: kills. Every enemy you slay grants a Leech stack, and those stacks keep you standing — a simple, self-feeding loop that turns crowded dungeons into a buffet.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/parasitic-stone.webp" alt="The coffin-style chest holding the Parasitic Stone" loading="eager" /><figcaption>A coffin-chest at the bottom of the Martyr's Tomb holds a very hungry stone.</figcaption></div>

## How to get it

1. **Open the map and fast travel to the Widow's Overlook Beacon.**
2. **Set out for the Martyr's Tomb dungeon** below the Overlook.
3. **Clear the enemies along the descent** — the tomb does not let you sneak past all of them, and you will want the practice anyway.
4. **Deep in the dungeon, find the coffin-style chest** and open it. The Parasitic Stone is inside.

## What it does

- **Every kill grants Leech stacks**, each restoring health — the core loop of the stone.
- **Upgrades raise the stacks earned per kill**, compounding the heal rate as the stone levels.

## Where it fits

The Parasitic Stone rewards the same lifestyle as the [Unwieldly Stone](/collectibles/unwieldly-stone) but with no activation cost — passive healing for players already slaughtering their way through packs. Run it alongside area weapons and watch entire rooms become a regen tick.
