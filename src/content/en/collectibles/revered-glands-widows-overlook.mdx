Revered Glands are the first upgrade every Mortal Shell II player should chase: consumed at any Beacon, each one permanently increases how many healing charges your **Mether's Pulse** flask holds. More charges means more mistakes forgiven — and in a game this aggressive, that is worth more than almost any weapon.

## What Revered Glands do — and what they don't

- **They raise your flask capacity.** Each gland consumed at a Beacon adds a use of the Mether's Pulse, the game's healing item, which refills whenever you rest.
- **They do not make each heal bigger.** Capacity and per-heal power are separate stats — this one is pure charges. For stronger individual heals, you want [Synaptic Vessels](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-marrow-keep) instead.
- **Pickups don't auto-apply.** A gland in your pocket does nothing until you spend it at a Beacon, so bank them deliberately.

## Widow's Overlook gland location

1. **Fast travel to the Widow's Overlook Beacon.**
2. **Walk into the woods behind the Beacon** and keep going for a stretch.
3. **Look to your right for a barren patch with a stone shrine** — a cluster of ritual stones, the largest standing tallest.
4. **A heavy-set corpse lies beneath the biggest stone. Loot it for the Revered Gland.**

Pair this with the [Revered Gland sold by the Marrow Keep merchant](/collectibles/revered-glands-marrow-keep) and the [one in the Sunken Village](/collectibles/revered-glands-sunken-village) and your flask grows by three early — enough to change how every early boss fight feels.
