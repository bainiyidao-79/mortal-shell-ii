Not every Revered Gland hides in a shrine. One of them is sitting in a shop inventory in Marrow Keep, waiting for anyone with the coins — which makes it the most reliable healing upgrade in Mortal Shell II.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/revered-glands-marrow-keep.webp" alt="The merchant's stall near the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The Marrow Keep merchant sells a Revered Gland outright.</figcaption></div>

## How to reach the merchant

1. **From the Marrow Keep Beacon, take the stairs on the left** and walk along until you spot the **Gloom Siphon** device.
2. **Turn right at the Gloom Siphon** — the merchant's stall is right there.
3. **Browse his wares and buy the Revered Gland.** It appears alongside other consumables; if you are coin-rich after early exploring, this is the first purchase worth making.

## Why buy this one first

Healing charges compound: every additional flask use pays off in every single fight for the rest of the run, while weapon upgrades mostly help one build at a time. Coins, meanwhile, can be farmed — so the gland's price is really just a small detour.

Round out your flask with the [Widow's Overlook shrine gland](/collectibles/revered-glands-widows-overlook) and the [Sunken Village pickup](/collectibles/revered-glands-sunken-village), then start on [Synaptic Vessels](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-marrow-keep) to fatten each individual heal.
