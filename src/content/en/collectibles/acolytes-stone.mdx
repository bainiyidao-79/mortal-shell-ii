The Acolyte's Stone gives your weapon a new verb: a charged light attack, powered by Resolve, that scales into a genuine critical-strike engine. No trial, no boss — it simply costs 2,000 coins at the Marrow Keep shop.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/acolytes-stone.webp" alt="Merrick's shop counter in Marrow Keep selling the Acolyte's Stone" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Two thousand coins buys your weapon a whole new attack.</figcaption></div>

## How to buy it

1. **Travel to Marrow Keep** and find **Merrick**, the merchant NPC.
2. **Talk to him and open the Tarstones tab** of his shop.
3. **Select the Acolyte's Stone and pay 2,000 coins.**

## What it does

- **Activation:** spend **40 Resolve** to unleash a charged light attack.
- **Level 1:** the charged attack gains **+15% critical chance**.
- **Level 2:** it deals **150% critical damage**.
- **Level 3:** the **Resolve cost drops to 30**, making the move spam-friendly.

## Why it is worth the coins

Charged attacks slot into any rhythm: open a duel with one, answer a boss's recovery with another, or bank them as burst between parry punishes. The crit scaling means it rewards every other crit-boosting piece you own, and the level 3 discount turns it from a finisher into a habit.

If your style runs heavier, its sibling is the [Unwieldly Stone](/collectibles/unwieldly-stone) — the same idea, built around charged heavy attacks and kill-time healing.
