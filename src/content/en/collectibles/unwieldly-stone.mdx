The Unwieldly Stone is the survival Tarstone: a charged heavy attack powered by Resolve, plus lifesteal stacks that accumulate with every kill. For heavy-weapon builds that live in the thick of packs, it converts aggression directly into health.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/unwieldly-stone.webp" alt="The Unwieldly Stone listed in Merrick's Tarstone shop" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Kills become healing — that is the whole business model.</figcaption></div>

## How to buy it

1. **Head to Marrow Keep** and speak with the merchant **Merrick**.
2. **Open the Tarstones tab** of his shop menu.
3. **Select the Unwieldly Stone** and pay **2,000 coins** with a long-press confirm.

## What it does

- **Activation:** spend **35 Resolve** on a charged heavy attack.
- **On kills:** gain **Leech stacks — up to five** — each restoring health, massively extending your endurance in pack-heavy areas.
- **Upgrades** raise the stacks earned per kill, so the snowball gets fatter as you invest.

## Who it is for

Pair it with big, slow, crowd-clearing weapons — the [Obsidian Hammer](/weapons/obsidian-hammer) or the [Great Martyr's Blade](/weapons/great-martyrs-blade) — where every charged swing threatens a posture break and every group is a full heal waiting to happen. Greedy front-line play stops being a mistake and becomes your regen rotation.

For a faster, crit-flavored take on the same shop tab, see the [Acolyte's Stone](/collectibles/acolytes-stone).
