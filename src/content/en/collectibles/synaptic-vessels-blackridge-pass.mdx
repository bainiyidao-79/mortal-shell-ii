Most Synaptic Vessels are looted quietly. The Blackridge Pass one has a doorman: a boss waiting at the bottom of a pit inside the Flooded Village dungeon. Kill it and the vessel — a permanent boost to every Mether's Pulse heal — is yours.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-blackridge-pass.webp" alt="The pit boss guarding a Synaptic Vessel in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The pit's resident boss is the only thing between you and a stronger flask.</figcaption></div>

## The route

1. **Start at the Blackridge Pass Beacon** and follow the stairs down a stretch.
2. **Look for the stone doorway with a transparent floor inside** — stepping on it drops you into the underground passage.
3. **Follow the tunnel until you reach a mechanism** — step on it to call the elevator.
4. **When the elevator stops, move forward; a second elevator hides behind a pillar.** Take it down toward the Flooded Village.
5. **Walk a stretch until you see the pit on your right** — the boss waits inside it.
6. **Kill the boss and collect the Synaptic Vessel** it guards.

## Fight notes

The arena is a literal hole, which works in your favor: the boss's approach angles are predictable, and ranged chip from the pit's rim is free. If the fight sours, the elevator loop back to the Beacon is short — heal, restock, drop again.
