The Sunken Village gland is the easiest of the three early Revered Glands: no shrine puzzle, no shop tab, just a staircase and a pickup. Here is the thirty-second version.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/revered-glands-sunken-village.webp" alt="The staircase in the Sunken Village hiding a Revered Gland" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Bottom of the stairs, right beside them — a free flask charge.</figcaption></div>

## Where to look

1. **Start from the Sunken Village Beacon** in the Glutted Mire.
2. **Head down the slope toward the staircase** visible below the spawn point.
3. **The Revered Gland sits right beside the stairs.** Grab it, cleanse the local Beacon if you haven't, and be on your way.

## Spend it, don't hoard it

Like every gland it only becomes a healing charge once consumed at a Beacon — and there is one right next to you. Use it on the spot, then continue the sweep with the [Widow's Overlook shrine gland](/collectibles/revered-glands-widows-overlook) and the [Marrow Keep merchant's stock](/collectibles/revered-glands-marrow-keep).
