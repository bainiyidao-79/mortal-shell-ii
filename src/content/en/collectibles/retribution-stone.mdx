If your Mortal Shell II game plan is parry, punish, repeat, the Retribution Stone is printed for you. This Tarstone upgrades **riposte damage** — the punishing counter that follows a successful parry — scaling from +10% at level 1 to +20% at level 2.

## How to get it

The Retribution Stone is a trial reward — no boss, no chest hunt:

1. **Travel to the Widow's Overlook area** and find the **trial stone tablet** marked on your map.
2. **Approach and hold the interact key** to begin the trial challenge.
3. **Complete the trial's combat challenge.** Success pays out immediately — the Retribution Stone is the reward.

## What it does

- **Level 1: +10% riposte damage.**
- **Level 2: +20% riposte damage.**

Equip it on a parry-focused build and every correctly timed block becomes a bigger statement. It pairs naturally with shells and weapons that reward standing your ground — and it stacks its way through the whole mid-game, since parry windows never fall off.

## Tarstone basics, briefly

Tarstones infuse into your equipment for passive and action-changing effects, leveling up as you slay enemies while equipped. Trials, dungeons and vendors all hand them out — the [Arbiter's Prize](/collectibles/arbiters-prize) and the shop-only [Acolyte's Stone](/collectibles/acolytes-stone) are two more worth an early detour.
