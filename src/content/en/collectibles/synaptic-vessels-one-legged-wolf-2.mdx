The second One-Legged Wolf vessel is a proper little dungeon crawl: hidden floors, a subterranean elevator and one spiteful wooden trap. The prize is the same as every Synaptic Vessel — a permanent boost to each Mether's Pulse heal once consumed at a Beacon.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-one-legged-wolf-2.webp" alt="The hidden underground route to the second One-Legged Wolf vessel" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Transparent floor, hidden elevator, one trap — and a stronger flask.</figcaption></div>

## The route, step by step

1. **From the One Legged Wolf Beacon, take the slope up.**
2. **Follow the wooden walkway on your right**, then continue up the wooden stairs on the right-hand side.
3. **At the fork, go straight** — ignore the right-hand path for now. Later, take that right and follow it to a **stone arch**.
4. **Pass under the arch and check behind the rightmost stone:** a **transparent floor panel** hides there. Step on it to drop underground.
5. **Follow the tunnel to the elevator** and ride it down.
6. **Exit the elevator and take the right-hand path.**
7. **Watch the floor: a wooden trap section collapses underfoot.** Let it drop you — the fall is the shortcut.
8. **Loot the Synaptic Vessel at the bottom.**

As always, consume it at a Beacon rather than letting it sit in your inventory. Pair it with [vessel #1](/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-one-legged-wolf-1) and your flask heals dramatically harder.
