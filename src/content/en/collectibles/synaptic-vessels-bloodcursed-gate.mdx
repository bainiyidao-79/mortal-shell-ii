The last of the five Synaptic Vessels is the simplest: from the Bloodcursed Gate Beacon — the gate Beacon on the road into the Sanguine Caverns — it is a straight walk along the marked road. No dungeon, no boss, no hidden floors.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/collectibles/synaptic-vessels-bloodcursed-gate.webp" alt="The road from the Bloodcursed Gate Beacon to the Synaptic Vessel" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Follow the marked road from the gate Beacon and the vessel is on the way.</figcaption></div>

## Where to walk

1. **Start at the Bloodcursed Gate Beacon.**
2. **Follow the marker on the road straight ahead** — the route runs directly toward the pickup.
3. **Grab the Synaptic Vessel where the marker ends.**

## Why this one matters

With this vessel your healing economy is complete: five vessels raising per-heal power on top of the three early [Revered Glands](/collectibles/revered-glands-widows-overlook) adding charges. Consumed together at a Beacon, they transform how many mistakes the mid-game bosses forgive.
