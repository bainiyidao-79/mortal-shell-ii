## What is the Devout Edition?

Mortal Shell II sells in three tiers: the **standard edition** ($49.99 at launch per Steam's listing), the **Devout Edition**, and the **Revered Edition**. The Devout Edition is the **mid-tier** — the middle ground between the base game and the top-shelf Revered package.

It's available on **Steam** (PC) and on **PlayStation 5**. Store listings and community references put it around the **$60** mark, though prices vary by platform and region — always treat the official Steam or PlayStation Store page as the source of truth for current pricing and exact contents.

## Is it worth it?

The community has been candid about the buying decision. A popular r/MortalShell thread is titled **"I preordered the Devout Edition and I'm not ashamed of it"** — the kind of post that says two things at once: the buyer is happy with the tier, and the community debates whether mid-tier preorders are "worth it" for a $50-game.

As with any deluxe tier, the honest calculus is simple:

- If you already know you'll sink tens of hours into the game (early impressions say exploration alone is substantial — see our [review roundup](/review/mortal-shell-ii-review)), the Devout tier's extras become easier to justify.
- If you're unsure, the standard edition is a complete game with no content gated behind editions — you can always [upgrade your understanding](/editions/mortal-shell-ii-revered-edition) of the top tier later.
