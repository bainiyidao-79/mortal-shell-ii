## What is the Revered Edition?

The **Revered Edition** is the **top tier** of Mortal Shell II's three release options, sitting above the standard edition ($49.99) and the [Devout Edition](/editions/mortal-shell-ii-devout-edition). Store and community references place it around the **$100** mark, which is consistent with a top-shelf collector's tier.

Cold Symmetry lists the Revered Edition prominently on the **official site (mortalshell.com)** with dedicated assets — including a **"Revered Edition on PS5"** section — making PlayStation 5 the platform where this tier is most visible. It also appears on Steam's edition selection for the PC version. As always, the official store pages are the source of truth for current pricing and the exact item list.

## Who is it for?

The Revered tier targets the superfans and collectors: players who already know they want everything the release has to offer. The community context supports that positioning — r/MortalShell threads about edition choices (like the popular "I preordered the Devout Edition and I'm not ashamed of it") show buyers consciously weighing tiers against playtime expectations.

A useful rule of thumb from early impressions of the game itself: Mortal Shell II rewards long exploration-heavy playthroughs — reviewer impressions put early sessions at 20+ hours with plenty left to find. The more hours you expect, the more sense a top tier makes. If you're on the fence, the standard edition is the complete game, and you can read our [review roundup](/review/mortal-shell-ii-review) before deciding.
