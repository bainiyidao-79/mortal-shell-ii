## Mortal Shell II released August 20, 2026

**Mortal Shell II is out now.** The game launched worldwide on **August 20, 2026** for PC (Steam, app ID 2584270) and PlayStation 5 — the official site's own headline changed to **"Now Available Worldwide"** at launch.

At launch, the standard edition was listed at **$49.99** on Steam, with the [Devout Edition](/editions/mortal-shell-ii-devout-edition) (~$60) and [Revered Edition](/editions/mortal-shell-ii-revered-edition) (~$100) above it.

## The road to launch

The release followed a busy 2026 for Cold Symmetry and publisher Playstack:

1. **Announcement** — the reveal came via an announcement trailer (the PlayStation channel's "Mortal Shell II - Announcement Trailer" passed **1.1 million views**), introducing the sequel's open-world direction.
2. **Console beta** — a dedicated "Console BETA Announcement" followed on the official Cold Symmetry channel, opening the game up to console players ahead of launch.
3. **Open beta** — an open beta launch trailer marked a public test window; the official site even kept a "PLAY THE BETA" call-to-action alongside the release messaging into launch week.
4. **Gameplay reveal** — IGN's "Official Gameplay Reveal" (705K+ views) and a 700-second gameplay deep-dive showed the open world, shells, and combat in extended form.
5. **Release date trailer** — the "Official Release Date Trailer 4k" on Cold Symmetry's channel locked in the August 20 date.
6. **Launch** — August 20, 2026, followed by a **"Global Launch Accolades Trailer"** on the official channel, rounding up early praise.
