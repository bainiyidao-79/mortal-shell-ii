## Mortal Shell II on PlayStation 5

Mortal Shell II launched on **August 20, 2026** for PlayStation 5 alongside the PC (Steam) release. The game lives on the PlayStation Store under concept ID **10010058** (store.playstation.com), and Sony's own channel gave the rollout a heavy push — the PlayStation-published "Mortal Shell II - Announcement Trailer | PS5 Games" passed **1.1 million views**, with separate announcement, launch, and release-date trailer cuts.

## PS5 Pro support

Cold Symmetry's official site (mortalshell.com) includes a dedicated **"Revered Edition on PS5"** section and references a **PS5 Pro** option, indicating the game ships with an enhanced mode for Sony's mid-generation console. Exact resolution/frame-rate targets per mode aren't something we can verify from primary sources yet — check the PS Store listing or the official site for the current spec claims.

## How the console rollout went

The console path to launch ran through dedicated test phases: Cold Symmetry posted a **"Console BETA Announcement"** on its official channel, followed by an **open beta** with its own launch trailer — meaning PS5 players got substantial hands-on time before day one. The launch was capped with a "Global Launch Accolades Trailer" collecting early praise.

## Is the PS5 version the one to get?

Both platforms run the same game; the choice is ecosystem and preference. Console considerations: you get the standard, tested console experience with the PS5 Pro enhancement option if you own that hardware.
