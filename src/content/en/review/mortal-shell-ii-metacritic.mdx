## Where to check the Metacritic score

Mortal Shell II has an official Metacritic page at **metacritic.com/game/mortal-shell-ii/**, which tracks critic reviews and user scores for both the PC and PS5 releases. Because scores update as reviews come in — and this site doesn't paste numbers we can't verify — the Metacritic page itself is the authoritative place to check the current rating.

## What critics are actually saying

While individual scores vary by outlet, the critical conversation around the August 20, 2026 launch has clear themes, consistent across major early reviews:

**What gets praised:**

- The **shell possession system** as the series' signature hook, now with more distinct playstyles
- **Open-world exploration** with dense secrets, illusory walls, and hand-placed finds
- The **art direction** — heavy metal, gothic, and H.R. Giger-inspired otherworldliness
- **Genre accessibility**: no stamina meter, forgiving parries, open-ended progression

**What draws criticism:**

- **Melee combat depth** — light/heavy combos stay simple over long sessions
- **Enemy variety** that feels similar in engagement despite good numbers
- **Uneven pacing** in some world regions and shrine placement

For the detailed breakdown behind these themes, see our [review roundup](/review/mortal-shell-ii-review), which quotes gameranx's full *Before You Buy* impressions (551K+ views) alongside other creator takes like Fextralife's "The BEST SOULSLIKE of ALL TIME ???" review.

## Context: Mortal Shell's review history

The first *Mortal Shell* (2020) was received as an "underrated, kind of experimental soulslike" — solid scores with recognition for its harden mechanic and shell system, and some criticism for brevity. Mortal Shell II's bigger open-world swing is precisely the response to that foundation, and it shapes what reviewers reward: ambition and world design over technical perfection.
