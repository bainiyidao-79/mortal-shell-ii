## Mortal Shell II on Steam

Mortal Shell II's PC home is **Steam, app ID 2584270** (store.steampowered.com/app/2584270). The game launched there on **August 20, 2026** at **$49.99** for the standard edition, with the [Devout Edition](/editions/mortal-shell-ii-devout-edition) and [Revered Edition](/editions/mortal-shell-ii-revered-edition) available as higher tiers on the same store page.

## Content descriptors

Steam carries an age gate with the developers' own content description: *"Mortal Shell II contains violence including sword fights with blood sprays, execution maneuvers, and medieval firearms."* That tracks with the game's aesthetic — heavy-metal-meets-gothic dark fantasy — and its ranged arsenal, which includes a shotgun-style firearm alongside bows and stranger options (see the [gameplay overview](/guide/mortal-shell-ii-gameplay)).

## Why PC is the tinkerer's platform

The Steam version is the one the PC tooling ecosystem targets:

- Review-copy coverage from major channels (gameranx's *Before You Buy* was captured on PC) means most footage you've seen is the Steam build

## Store page pointers

The Steam listing includes the standard edition selection, community hub, and news feed. Cold Symmetry uses the Steam news system for patches and updates — worth following post-launch alongside the official YouTube channel.

## PC vs PS5

Same game, same day-and-date launch (August 20, 2026). The PS5 version adds console conveniences and a PS5 Pro enhancement option ([details](/review/mortal-shell-ii-ps5)). The Steam version is where trainers and future mods live. If you're deciding between platforms, the honest summary from early reviews: the game is demanding on reactions but not on hardware specs at a baseline level — though exact PC system requirements should be checked on the store page, as we don't republish specs we can't verify.
