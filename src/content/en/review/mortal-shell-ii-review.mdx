## The short verdict

Early reviews of Mortal Shell II (released August 20, 2026) land on a consistent summary: a **recommended action RPG with standout exploration, art direction, and progression systems**, held back by shallow melee variety and some uneven stretches. As gameranx's Jake Baldino put it in *Before You Buy*: "I think Mortal Shell 2 is mostly a really good time, and I'm going to recommend it to some of you."

## The praise

From gameranx's detailed hands-on (551K+ views, review copy, spoiler-free):

- **Exploration is the star.** "You're constantly stumbling upon new, different-looking biomes, strange new lands, hidden crypts, illusory walls, and weird-ass NPCs." The open world "isn't the most massive ever, but they do a good job of making it feel big."
- **The shell system works.** Inhabiting dead warriors — each with distinct abilities like invisibility backstabs, grappling hooks, or self-generating flasks — made the reviewer "actively really want to hunt for stuff."
- **Art direction stands out.** "Heavy metal, medieval, gothic vibes and H.R. Giger otherworldly strangeness in a way that just really freaking works." Where other games feel generic mixing fantasy with the alien, "Mortal Shell 2 manages to stand out and be unique here."
- **Progression is deep.** Tar stones that level with use, weapon infusions at the tar forge, the resolve meter, and seals that rewrite your defense all give builds real texture.
- **It's accessible** for the genre: no stamina meter, generous parry timing, and an open world that lets you leave a wall you're stuck against.

Bigger channels echo the enthusiasm: **Fextralife's** review video asks "The BEST SOULSLIKE of ALL TIME ???" (225K+ views), and **IHaveInsipidus** posted "Mortal Shell 2 Is Incredible" after launch.

## The criticisms

The same sources are honest about flaws:

- **Melee lacks combo depth.** "Just simple heavy and light attacks" — satisfying to land, with great animations and gore, but the reviewer "got bored of my weapon" after long stretches, which pushed him into (expensive) weapon-swapping.
- **Enemy variety is decent but similar.** Enemies exist in number, yet "a lot of them feel kind of similar in terms of how you engage with them."
- **Uneven world density.** Some areas "feel kind of barren" between the interesting ones, and a few stretches have no shrines, making deaths tedious.
- **One-shot enemies and falls.** Some foes can one-shot you even with a warning, and falls "lost quite a bit of time" — annoying in an open world with elevation everywhere.

## The bottom line

gameranx's closing take captures the consensus: "It's not high art or obscure, fine-tuned, nerd-out RPG design... It's just a fun video game. And sometimes things can be that simple. Mortal Shell 2 is cool."
