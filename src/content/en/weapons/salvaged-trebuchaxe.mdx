The Salvaged Trebuchaxe throws entire axes at people, and it is exactly as fun as it sounds. This mid-range sidearm consumes Resolve to hurl a charging war-axe; a fully charged throw deletes most normal enemies outright, making it the hardest-hitting opening button of the early game.

## How to get it

1. **Start from the Gloomshade Grove Beacon** and head south.
2. **Defeat the world boss nearby — the Bloodcursed Lithopod.** Its defeat opens the way into the **Ravaged Hideout** dungeon.
3. **Work through the dungeon to the final room.** The Salvaged Trebuchaxe rests on a corpse there.

## What it plays like

- **Burst-pick perfection.** A full-charge throw kills most standard enemies in one hit — patrolling packs become a sequence of free, silent-ish removals.
- **Arcing drop.** The projectile falls over distance, so long-range accuracy takes practice; mid-range throws are the sweet spot.
- **Charging is exposed.** Winding up a throw takes time, and an enemy in your face makes the whole gesture a bad idea. Open fights with it, or reposition first — never charge it inside a swing's range.

Treat it as the punctuation mark of your build: dash in with your primary, disengage, and end the argument with a thrown axe.
