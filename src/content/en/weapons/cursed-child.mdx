The Cursed Child is the saddest weapon in Mortal Shell II — a spirit stranded between life and death that aches to be held, and defends whoever carries it with terrifying devotion. As a sidearm it summons a wandering ghost that tracks and attacks enemies on its own.

## The route in

1. **Fast travel to the monastery gate Beacon** and take the stairs to the north-west, toward the rolling-ball enemy's path.
2. **Cross to the western platform and drop down** to the lower ledge marked by the bone gate.
3. **Head north past the hammer-wielding enemy.** The entrance to the **Revered Beacon** dungeon is on your right.
4. **Dodge the golden beam.** Inside, the central tower fires a tracking beam that chases you and keeps damaging you until you break line of sight behind a wall. It locks onto enemies too — bait them into it when you can.
5. **Circle the area counterclockwise, climb to the tower, and attack the central pillar** to shut the beam off.
6. **Shatter the three statues and defeat the mini-boss** at the end. The Cursed Child is your reward.

## What it plays like

- **Fire and forget.** The summoned spirit seeks targets on its own, adding damage while you focus on dodging — a genuine second combatant.
- **Narrow rooms are the danger.** The dungeon on the way in is cramped and full of ghost-type enemies; getting surrounded there is the main way this trip goes wrong.
- **Pairs with defensive builds.** While the Child harasses, you harden, block and punish — pressure without exposure.
