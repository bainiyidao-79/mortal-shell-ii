The Forgotten Crossbow turns Mortal Shell II's ranged slot into a sniper's tool. Its range is enormous, headshots pay handsomely, and a few bolts are enough to collapse a normal enemy's posture into a free execution.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/weapons/forgotten-crossbow.webp" alt="The Forgotten Crossbow on a table in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Long range, heavy bolts, and posture damage that sets up executions.</figcaption></div>

## How to get it

1. **Fast travel to the Blackridge Pass Beacon** and head north-west.
2. **Pass under the stone arch** to enter the building, which leads into the **Flooded Village** dungeon.
3. **Ride the second elevator down** and work through the sunken village to its lower section.
4. **Behind you on the left, check the crates beside one of the houses** — the **Damp Key** rests on them.
5. **Use the Damp Key on the building at the center of the village.** The Forgotten Crossbow is waiting on the table inside.

## What it plays like

- **Opening-pick specialist.** Its reach lets you start fights before the enemy knows the fight has started — chip posture from safety, then close for the execution.
- **Headshot lottery.** Precision hits deal bonus damage, making the crossbow surprisingly lethal against elite-sized health bars when you take your time aiming.
- **Posture workhorse.** A handful of bolts is enough to stagger normal enemies outright, and it softens elite stance bars before you ever swing a melee weapon.

Ammo is plentiful enough that you can lean on it, but resolve-hungry players should still treat each shot as a decision, not a spray.
