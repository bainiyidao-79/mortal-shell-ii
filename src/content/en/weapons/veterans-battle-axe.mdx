The Veteran's Battle Axe is for players who like their numbers big and their commitments bigger. Found on a headless corpse at the very bottom of The King's Crypt, it hits like a siege engine — and swings like one too.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/weapons/veterans-battle-axe.webp" alt="The Veteran's Battle Axe in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Slow, enormous, and devastating when the timing lands.</figcaption></div>

## Where to find it

1. **Travel to the Shattered Beacon** and head north-west.
2. **Enter the cave** that leads into **The King's Crypt** dungeon.
3. **Follow the main path** until you reach a long, deep drop shaft — jump down.
4. **The axe lies on a headless corpse** at the bottom. An elite bandit stands right beside it, so clear the space before you loot.

## What it plays like

- **Huge per-hit damage.** Few weapons in Mortal Shell II hit harder per swing, and posture damage is similarly monstrous.
- **Slow on both ends.** Wind-ups and recoveries are long, attack frequency is low, and you cannot string quick hits together — plan each swing like a statement.
- **Stamina-hungry.** Chained slashes drain the bar fast; emptying yourself mid-swing is an invitation to be punished. Swing, recover, repeat.

Pair it with hardening to tank through your own recovery frames, or run it on a tanky Shell where one heavy read per exchange is enough to control the fight.
