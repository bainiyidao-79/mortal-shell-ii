The Axe and Dagger is the pairing an early sage practically hands you — and for good reason. Its chains come out fast, every finisher leaves you free to dodge away, and its dash-heavy heavy attack is one of the best damage-per-second tools of the entire opening game. The catch is reach: you must fight close, and polearm enemies will punish lazy spacing.

## What it plays like

- **Agile by design.** Every chain ends recoverable — you can bail out of a string and reposition better than with almost any other weapon, which suits hit-and-run aggression.
- **Strong early damage.** Its output holds up through the whole opening stretch, and the dash heavy attack remains a legitimate boss-fight tool long after early game ends.
- **Short reach is the tax.** Against long-handled enemies you must commit to gaps, or eat counter-hits for free. Base hits are light; the weapon lives on combos and that dashing heavy.

## How to get it: the Chapel Key route

1. **Fast travel to the Mushroom Village Beacon.**
2. **Head south** toward the area with the huge burning pyre, where enemies are camped.
3. **Find the stone archway on the north edge of the platform.** Walk straight through it facing the fire, and a statue is revealed.
4. **Take the Chapel Key hanging from the statue's rope.**
5. **Return to the village and head north-west**, where the Chapel Key opens the door to the **Shrine of Trials** dungeon.

## Inside the Shrine of Trials

The dungeon is short and mostly puzzle-flavored. In the first room, face the statue and **walk backwards** to pass through the hidden wall. The following rooms are simple block puzzles: strike the stone blocks with your weapon to shove them onto their pressure plates. Clear the final room's elite and the Axe and Dagger is yours.
