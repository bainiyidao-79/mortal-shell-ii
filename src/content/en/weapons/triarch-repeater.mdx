The Triarch Repeater is the sidearm for players who hate reloading: a rapid three-shot burst crossbow that needs no ammunition at all — every shot is paid in Resolve. Fast handling, quick bursts and excellent posture damage make it the most spammable ranged tool in Mortal Shell II.

## Where to find it — no boss required

The best part of the Triarch Repeater is the price of admission: you do not fight your way to the end of anything.

1. **Fast travel to the Castigator's Keep Beacon.**
2. **Follow the road down — or simply drop off the back of the Beacon platform** — to reach the lower area.
3. **Head north along the path** until you reach the cliff-corner entrance of the **Blackwell Cavern** dungeon.
4. **Push into the dungeon** — the Repeater is sitting on the ground in the **first lit area, roughly mid-way through**. Pick it up and walk out.

## What it plays like

- **Burst pressure on demand.** Three quick bolts per trigger pull shred posture and interrupt wind-ups; a burst at the right moment cancels more attacks than any other sidearm.
- **Ammo-free by design.** Because it burns Resolve instead of bolts, aggressive play that keeps landing melee swings literally refills its magazine.
- **Sustained ranged DPS.** Against staggered or fleeing enemies, back-to-back bursts finish fights that other sidearms would need reloading for.

If the [Forgotten Crossbow](/weapons/forgotten-crossbow) is the sniper rifle, the Triarch Repeater is the SMG — and the two come from conveniently adjacent regions.
