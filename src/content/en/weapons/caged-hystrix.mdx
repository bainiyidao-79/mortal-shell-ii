The Caged Hystrix weaponizes a small, suffering creature in a cage: throw it, and the pod detonates when an enemy wanders too close, dealing sustained area damage. It is less a gunshot and more a landmine — the sidearm of players who fight on their own terms.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/weapons/caged-hystrix.webp" alt="The Caged Hystrix trap sidearm in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Throw it down, step back, and let curiosity do the rest.</figcaption></div>

## How to get it — same trip as the Clockwork Scythe

The Caged Hystrix comes from the Sariel boss line at The Silent Steps:

1. **Fast travel to The Silent Steps Beacon** and go south-east to the large stone platform where **Sariel, the Endless** waits.
2. **Beat him once.** He slips through a wall that opens into the **Chamber of Becoming** dungeon.
3. **Chase him through and win the rematch**, destroying all four grave markers so he stays down.
4. **Do not leave yet.** Climb the stairs at the back of the final room — the Caged Hystrix sits near the dungeon exit.

## What it plays like

- **Area denial.** A thrown pod turns a doorway, choke point or boss-arena corner into punished ground — enemies who step in eat continuous damage.
- **Defense-first design.** It shines when you are buying space: drop a pod, retreat, and let anything chasing you walk into the blast.
- **Patience over spam.** Like all sidearms it runs on Resolve, and its value comes from placement, not volume. One well-placed pod per exchange is plenty.
