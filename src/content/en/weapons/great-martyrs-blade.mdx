The Great Martyr's Blade is a two-handed greatsword with some of the best single-hit damage in Mortal Shell II. Enormous reach, wide sweeps, and elite-clearing power — and claiming it unlocks the fittingly named Big Boi achievement.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/weapons/great-martyrs-blade.webp" alt="The Great Martyr's Blade resting in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Top-tier single hits and sweeping arcs, straight from the Martyr's Prison altar.</figcaption></div>

## The route in

1. **Start from the Gloomshade Grove Beacon** and head north-west, following the road to the fortress's outer north-east corner.
2. **Look for the breach in the wall** — that hole is the entrance to the **Martyr's Prison** dungeon. (Coming from the back entrance of the prison dungeon near the well works too.)
3. **Inside, interact with the sack on the stone platform** to zip across to another section of the dungeon.
4. **Keep pushing forward until you reach a locked room holding four enemies.** Kill all four and the door to the weapon room opens.
5. **Claim the blade from the altar** at the heart of the dungeon.

## What it plays like

- **Elite single-target damage.** Per-hit numbers sit at the top of the weapon list, and the reach lets you start swings from outside most enemy threat ranges.
- **Wide horizontal sweeps** make crowd control genuinely viable — rare for a weapon this heavy.
- **The price is speed.** It is one of the slowest weapons in the game, and without planning its rhythm gets you clipped on recoveries. Harden through punishes, or pair it with Frost-style builds that make every landed hit count.

If you enjoy heavy weapons but find the Veteran's Battle Axe too stamina-starved, the Great Martyr's Blade is the natural next step: same commitment, far bigger payoff.
