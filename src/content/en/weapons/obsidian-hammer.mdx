The Obsidian Hammer is what happens when a weapon stops asking politely. A two-handed chunk of obsidianite with the highest single-hit impact and posture damage in Mortal Shell II, its heavy attacks crush enemy stance outright and force executions.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/weapons/obsidian-hammer.webp" alt="The Obsidian Hammer in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Highest impact in the game — every heavy swing threatens a posture break.</figcaption></div>

## Where to find it

1. **From the Outskirts of Mammon Beacon, head south-west** along the road.
2. **Cross the wooden bridge** into the scaffold-caked area known as the **Deserted Slums**.
3. **In the center of the slums, drop down** to the lower level where miners are working the rock.
4. **The entrance to the Obsidianite Mines dungeon** sits at the south-west side of that pit.
5. **Push through to the end of the dungeon** and defeat the mini-boss — the **Prophet** — and the hammer comes to you automatically.

## What it plays like

- **Posture destruction.** The whole identity of the weapon: heavy attacks collapse enemy stance and open executions against almost anything, including armored elites.
- **A shockwave heavy.** The dashing heavy attack is a massive downward slam with area tremor — superb against large bosses and heavily armored foes, and it catches groups too.
- **Crowds fear it.** Few weapons clear a packed room as safely: one well-placed slam staggers everything nearby, and staggered enemies stay honest while you finish them one by one.

It arrives mid-to-late game, which suits its weight class — this is the tool you bring to the fights that were giving you trouble.
