The Ballistazooka does not do subtle. This heavy sidearm launches giant explosive bolts that detonate for area damage — devastating against packed groups and staggered elites, and expensive enough with Resolve that every trigger pull is a commitment.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/weapons/ballistazooka.webp" alt="The Ballistazooka heavy sidearm in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>One bolt, one explosion, several opinions changed.</figcaption></div>

## Where to find it

1. **Fast travel to the Gate of Mammon Beacon** and head north-west.
2. **Climb the first staircase into the building's ground floor**, then circle the structure clockwise into its interior.
3. **Take the stairs down into the Sentry's Grave dungeon.**
4. **Fight through to the end and defeat the mini-boss — the Subjugated Guardian.** The Ballistazooka is yours automatically when it falls.

## What it plays like

- **Real area damage.** The explosion hits everything near the impact — groups, pairs, and any enemy hiding behind the one you aimed at.
- **Resolve-expensive.** This is the price of the power: the cost per shot is the highest among sidearms, so spamming is not a strategy. Land swings with your primary to refill, then spend big.
- **Executioner's tool.** Its best role is ending wounded elites and scattering reinforcements the moment they cluster — one bolt converts a messy brawl into a series of duels.

Bring it to fights with adds and thank yourself later.
