The Bard's Lute is the strangest sidearm in Mortal Shell II: a magical instrument whose blasts punish enemies and confuse them into turning on each other. And unlike most of the arsenal, it asks for no blood — it is a stage pickup in a tavern.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/weapons/bards-lute.webp" alt="The Bard's Lute on the tavern stage in Mortal Shell II" loading="eager" /><figcaption>On the stage, beside the house musician — free for whoever appreciates chaos.</figcaption></div>

## Where to find it

1. **Fast travel to the One Legged Wolf Beacon.**
2. **Head south-east into the One-Legged Wolf tavern.**
3. **Walk to the stage where the musician is playing** — the lute sits right beside him on the stage. Pick it up. No boss, no dungeon, no fight.

## The gate in front of the gate

The only complication is reaching the tavern at all: the area is sealed behind corruption that only the **Mether's Breath** ability clears. You earn Mether's Breath by defeating a main gate boss and delivering its Ova, so if the tavern wall refuses you, go win a gate first and come back.

## What it plays like

- **Confusion is crowd control.** Lute blasts can turn enemies against each other, letting a surrounded Harbinger turn a mob into a bar brawl that does not involve him.
- **A real damage option.** The magical bolts hit respectably, giving resolve-heavy builds a genuine ranged pressure tool.
- **Perfect for hit-and-run kits.** Pair it with mobile weapons like the [Axe and Dagger](/weapons/axe-and-dagger): soften and scramble from range, then dash in for the cleanup.
