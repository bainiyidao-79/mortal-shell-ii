The Clockwork Scythe earns its reputation. Its light chains are fluid and mid-speed, its heavy attack is a wide spinning slash that covers angles most weapons cannot touch, and it stacks curse on enemies with miserable consistency. Elites and armored foes hate all of it.

## How to get it: the Sariel line

The scythe drops from the same repeat boss line as the Sariel Shell:

1. **Fast travel to The Silent Steps Beacon** and head south-east to the large stone platform.
2. **Defeat Sariel, the Endless** in the first encounter. He escapes through a wall that grinds open, revealing the **Chamber of Becoming** dungeon.
3. **Chase him through the dungeon** to the final room.
4. **Smash all four grave markers** — the glowing plates that revive him — then kill him properly. The Clockwork Scythe unlocks automatically.

While you are there, climb the stairs at the back of the final room: the [Caged Hystrix](/weapons/caged-hystrix) sidearm sits near the exit, making this one trip pay three times.

## What it plays like

- **Smooth, reliable chains.** Mid-speed swings link cleanly, so curse stacks and posture chip accumulate without risky commitments.
- **The spinning heavy.** A broad rotation that covers your sides — flankers who dodge around your lights run straight into it.
- **Curse pressure.** Steady affliction build-up turns long fights into timed fights: the enemy's health bar keeps melting even between your swings.
